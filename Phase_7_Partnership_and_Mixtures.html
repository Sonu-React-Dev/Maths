📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Partnership Profit Ratio
P₁ : P₂ : P₃ = (I₁ × T₁) : (I₂ × T₂) : (I₃ × T₃)
💡 Profit is directly proportional to Capital × Duration
Alligation Formula
Quantity₁ / Quantity₂ = (C_Dearer - C_Mean) / (C_Mean - C_Cheaper)
💡 Mean concentration is strictly between C₁ and C₂
Successive Dilution
Remaining Liquid = Initial Volume × [1 - (x / V)]ⁿ
💡 x = replaced amount, V = total volume, n = repetitions

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Dilution Formula
Q: A container contains 80 litres of milk. 8 litres is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated twice more. Find remaining milk.
Solution:
Initial = 80, x = 8, n = 3.
Remaining = 80 × [1 - 8/80]³ = 80 × (9/10)³ = 80 × 0.729 = 58.32 litres.
Alligation on CP
Q: In what ratio must rice at ₹60/kg be mixed with rice at ₹85/kg so that selling the mixture at ₹84/kg gives 20% profit?
Solution:
Mean CP = 84 / 1.2 = ₹70/kg.
Alligation:
(85 - 70) : (70 - 60) = 15 : 10 = 3 : 2.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: A and B invest in ratio 3:5. After 4 months, A increases capital by 50% and B withdraws 20%. At end of year, find ratio of profits.
Optimal Solution:
A = (3 × 4) + (4.5 × 8) = 12 + 36 = 48.
B = (5 × 4) + (4 × 8) = 20 + 32 = 52.
Ratio = 48 : 52 = 12 : 13.
