# PHASE 1 — Number System (Days 6–12)

### 📌 Overview & Target
- **Days:** 6 to 12 (7 Days)
- **Exam Weightage:** High (3–5 Questions in Tier-1 & Tier-2)
- **Topics:**
  - Number classification: Natural, Whole, Integers, Rational, Irrational, Prime, Composite.
  - Divisibility Rules: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 72, 88, 99.
  - HCF and LCM: Prime factorization, Division method, Word problems, Remainder cases.
  - Unit Digit & Cyclicity: Cyclicity of 2, 3, 7, 8 (order 4); 4, 9 (order 2); 0, 1, 5, 6 (order 1).
  - Remainder Theorems: Basic remainder, Wilson's / Fermat's / Euler's concept overview.
  - Number of Factors: Total factors, even/odd factors, sum of factors.

### 📐 Key Formulas & Shortcuts
- **Two numbers relation:** $\text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = A \times B$ *(Caution: Only valid for two numbers!)*
- **Number of factors:** If $N = a^p \cdot b^q \cdot c^r$ (where $a, b, c$ are primes), then $\text{Total Factors} = (p+1)(q+1)(r+1)$.
- **Sum of factors:** $S = \frac{a^{p+1}-1}{a-1} \cdot \frac{b^{q+1}-1}{b-1} \cdot \frac{c^{r+1}-1}{c-1}$.
- **Divisibility of 11:** Difference between sum of digits at odd places and even places must be $0$ or multiple of $11$.

### 📐 Key Formulas Quick Reference
| Shape | Area / Volume | Perimeter / Surface Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equilateral $\Delta$** | $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2$ | $3a$, Height $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}a$ |
| **Rhombus** | $\frac{1}{2} d_1 d_2$ | $4a$, where $4a^2 = d_1^2 + d_2^2$ |
| **Trapezium** | $\frac{1}{2} (a+b) h$ | Sum of all 4 sides |
| **Cylinder** | $V = \pi r^2 h$ | $\text{CSA} = 2\pi r h$, $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r+h)$ |
| **Cone** | $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$ | $\text{CSA} = \pi r l$, $\text{TSA} = \pi r(r+l)$ |
