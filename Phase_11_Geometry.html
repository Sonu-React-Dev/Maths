📅 Days 73–78 (6 Days) — Day-by-Day Conceptual Master Plan

Day 73
Lines, Angles & Transversal Theorems
📝 20 Geometric Drills
Complementary/Supplementary Angles, Parallel Line Pairs & Bisector Geometry
  • Angle Angle Fundamentals: • Complementary (पूरक कोण): $\alpha + \beta = 90^\circ$.
    • Supplementary (संपूरक कोण): $\alpha + \beta = 180^\circ$.
    Example: An angle is $24^\circ$ more than its complement $\implies \theta - (90 - \theta) = 24 \implies 2\theta = 114 \implies \theta = 57^\circ$.
  • Parallel Lines Intersected by a Transversal: When lines $L_1 \parallel L_2$ are cut by transversal $T$:
    • Corresponding Angles (संगत कोण): Equal ($\angle 1 = \angle 5$).
    • Alternate Interior Angles (एकांतर अंतः कोण): Equal ($\angle 3 = \angle 5$, forming a "Z" shape).
    • Consecutive Interior Angles (क्रमागत अंतः कोण): Sum to $180^\circ$ ($\angle 3 + \angle 6 = 180^\circ$, forming a "C" shape).
  • Interior Angle Bisectors Theorem: The angle bisectors of two consecutive interior angles between parallel lines ALWAYS meet at an angle of $\mathbf{90^\circ}$!
  • The Arrow / Reflex Vertex Shortcut: In a reflex vertex between parallel lines, the sum of acute angles facing left equals the sum of acute angles facing right: $\mathbf{\theta = \alpha + \beta}$.
🎯 Day 73 Focus: Look for the "Z" shape (alternate equal) and "C" shape (sum 180°) in parallel line diagrams.
Day 74
Triangles — Inequalities & Congruence
📝 25 Triangle Drills
Exterior Angle Theorem, Triangle Inequality & Type Classification
  • Core Angle & Exterior Theorems: • Sum of interior angles $= 180^\circ$.
    • Exterior Angle Theorem: An exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles: $\mathbf{\angle ACD = \angle A + \angle B}$.
  • The Triangle Inequality Theorem (Side Existence): A triangle with sides $a, b, c$ can exist IF AND ONLY IF: $$\mathbf{|a - b| < c < a + b}$$ The sum of any two sides is strictly greater than the third side, and the difference is strictly less.
  • Acute, Right & Obtuse Classification: Let $c$ be the largest side of $\triangle ABC$ ($c \ge a, b$):
    • $\mathbf{a^2 + b^2 > c^2} \implies$ Acute-angled Triangle.
    • $\mathbf{a^2 + b^2 = c^2} \implies$ Right-angled Triangle (Pythagoras).
    • $\mathbf{a^2 + b^2 < c^2} \implies$ Obtuse-angled Triangle.
  • Isosceles & Equilateral Properties: • In an equilateral triangle of side $a$: $\text{Height } h = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}a$, $\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2$.
    • If interior point $P$ has perpendiculars $p_1, p_2, p_3$ to the three sides: $\mathbf{h = p_1 + p_2 + p_3 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}a}$.
🎯 Day 74 Focus: For equilateral triangle with internal perpendiculars $p_1, p_2, p_3$, side $a = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}(p_1 + p_2 + p_3)$.
Day 75
Similarity, Thales & Apollonius Theorem
📝 25 Similarity Qs
Basic Proportionality (Thales), Area-Side Square Law & Median Power
  • Thales Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem): If $DE \parallel BC$ in $\triangle ABC$, then: $$\mathbf{\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}} \quad \Big| \quad \mathbf{\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{DE}{BC}}$$
  • The Area-Similarity Square Law: If $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$ with similarity scale ratio $k = \frac{AB}{DE}$: $$\mathbf{\frac{\text{Area}(\triangle ABC)}{\text{Area}(\triangle DEF)} = \left(\frac{AB}{DE}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{h_1}{h_2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{m_1}{m_2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{P_1}{P_2}\right)^2}$$ Example: If side ratio is $3 : 5 \implies$ Perimeter ratio is $3 : 5 \implies$ Area ratio is $3^2 : 5^2 = 9 : 25$!
  • Apollonius Theorem (Median Length Formula): In $\triangle ABC$, if $AD$ is the median to side $BC$ ($BD = DC$): $$\mathbf{AB^2 + AC^2 = 2(AD^2 + BD^2)}$$ Example: $AB = 6, AC = 8, BC = 10 \implies BD = 5$.
    $6^2 + 8^2 = 2(AD^2 + 5^2) \implies 100 = 2(AD^2 + 25) \implies AD^2 = 25 \implies AD = 5\text{ cm}$.
  • Interior Angle Bisector Theorem: If $AD$ bisects $\angle A$ meeting $BC$ at $D$: $\mathbf{\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}}$. The bisector divides opposite base in ratio of adjacent sides!
🎯 Day 75 Focus: Angle bisector divides the base in the ratio of adjacent sides ($\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}$).
Day 76
The 4 Triangle Centres & Euler Line
📝 25 Centre Angle Drills
Incenter, Circumcenter, Orthocenter, Centroid & Their Angles
  • 1. Incenter ($I$ / अंतःकेंद्र): Concurrency of Angle Bisectors. Equidistant from all 3 sides. $$\mathbf{\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac{\angle A}{2}} \quad \Big| \quad \text{Inradius } \mathbf{r = \frac{\Delta}{s}}$$ (External angle bisectors meeting: $\angle B I_{\text{ext}} C = 90^\circ - \frac{\angle A}{2}$).
  • 2. Circumcenter ($O$ / परिकेंद्र): Concurrency of Perpendicular Bisectors of Sides. Equidistant from vertices. $$\mathbf{\angle BOC = 2\angle A} \quad \Big| \quad \text{Circumradius } \mathbf{R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta}}$$ • In Right Triangle: $R = \frac{\text{Hypotenuse}}{2}$ (Circumcenter lies at the midpoint of hypotenuse!).
  • 3. Orthocenter ($H$ / लंबकेंद्र): Concurrency of Altitudes. $$\mathbf{\angle BHC = 180^\circ - \angle A}$$ • In Right Triangle: Orthocenter lies exactly at the right-angled vertex!
  • 4. Centroid ($G$ / केंद्रक): Concurrency of Medians. • Divides each median in ratio $\mathbf{2 : 1}$ (vertex to base).
    • The 3 medians divide the triangle into $\mathbf{6\text{ triangles of EQUAL AREA}}$.
    • Euler Line: Orthocenter ($H$), Centroid ($G$), and Circumcenter ($O$) are collinear: $\mathbf{H - G - O}$ in ratio $\mathbf{2 : 1}$.
🎯 Day 76 Focus: Instantly distinguish: Incenter $\angle BIC = 90^\circ + A/2$, Circumcenter $\angle BOC = 2A$, Orthocenter $\angle BHC = 180^\circ - A$.
Day 77
Circle Theorems & Tangents Mastery
📝 30 Chord/Tangent Drills
Chords, Cyclic Quadrilaterals, Power of Point & Common Tangents
  • Core Circle Angle Theorems: • Angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle at the circumference: $\angle AOB = 2\angle ACB$.
    • Angles in the same segment are equal.
    • Angle in a semicircle is a right angle ($\mathbf{90^\circ}$).
  • Cyclic Quadrilateral (चक्रीय चतुर्भुज): • Opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$: $\mathbf{\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ}, \mathbf{\angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ}$.
    • Exterior angle equals the opposite interior angle.
  • Chord Theorems & Power of Point: • Intersecting Chords (Internal/External): $\mathbf{PA \times PB = PC \times PD}$.
    • Tangent-Secant Theorem: $\mathbf{PT^2 = PA \times PB}$. (Where $PT$ is tangent, $PAB$ is secant).
  • Alternate Segment Theorem: Angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle subtended by that chord in the opposite alternate segment.
  • Common Tangents to Two Circles (Distance $d$): • Direct Common Tangent: $\mathbf{\text{DCT} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2}}$.
    • Transverse Common Tangent: $\mathbf{\text{TCT} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2}}$.
🎯 Day 77 Focus: In $PT^2 = PA \times PB$, remember $PB$ is the full external line segment, not just the internal chord $AB$.
Day 78
Polygons, Regular Hexagon & Phase Test
📝 25 Qs in 20 Minutes
Regular Polygon Formulas, Hexagon Equilateral Grid & Timed Mastery
  • Universal Polygon Formulas ($n$ Sides): • Sum of all interior angles: $\mathbf{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}$.
    • Each interior angle of regular polygon: $\mathbf{\frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n}}$.
    • Sum of all exterior angles: ALWAYS $\mathbf{360^\circ}$.
    • Each exterior angle: $\mathbf{\frac{360^\circ}{n}}$. (Shortcut: Interior $= 180^\circ - \text{Exterior}$).
    • Number of Diagonals: $\mathbf{\frac{n(n - 3)}{2}}$. (e.g. Decagon $n=10 \implies \frac{10 \times 7}{2} = 35$ diagonals).
  • Regular Hexagon ($n=6$): Consists of $\mathbf{6\text{ identical equilateral triangles}}$ of side $a$:
    $\mathbf{\text{Area} = 6 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^2}$.
    Long diagonal $= 2a$  |  Short diagonal $= \sqrt{3}a$.
  • Phase 11 Timed Mastery Protocol: 25 mixed Geometry questions under 20-minute timer. Target: $\ge 85\%$ accuracy.
🎯 Day 78 Focus: Find regular polygon sides instantly: $n = \frac{360^\circ}{\text{Exterior Angle}}$.

📐 Formula Vault

📐 Four Triangle Centers & Angle Theorems
\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac{\angle A}{2}  |  \angle BOC = 2\angle A  |  \angle BHC = 180^\circ - \angle A  |  AG : GD = 2 : 1
When to use: Incenter ($I$, angle bisectors), Circumcenter ($O$, $\perp$ bisectors), Orthocenter ($H$, altitudes), Centroid ($G$, medians).
⚡ Shortcut: If $\angle A = 80^\circ$: $\angle BIC = 90 + 40 = 130^\circ$, $\angle BOC = 160^\circ$, $\angle BHC = 180 - 80 = 100^\circ$ in 2 seconds!
🚨 Trap: Centroid has NO angle formula; it divides each median in $2:1$ from vertex to base. Don't invent angle formulas for centroid!
📐 Tangent-Secant & Intersecting Chords
PT^2 = PA \times PB  |  PA \times PB = PC \times PD  |  \text{DCT} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2}  |  \text{TCT} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2}
When to use: Tangent $PT$ and secant $PAB$ from external point $P$; common tangents between two circles.
⚡ Shortcut: If $PT = 12$ and $PA = 8 \implies PB = 144/8 = 18 \implies$ Chord $AB = 18 - 8 = 10$ cm immediately!
🚨 Trap: $PB$ is total secant length ($PA + AB$), NOT chord $AB$. $PT^2 = PA \times AB$ is a catastrophic error!
📐 Similarity & Thales Theorem (Area vs Side)
\frac{\text{Area}_1}{\text{Area}_2} = \left(\frac{\text{Side}_1}{\text{Side}_2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{P_1}{P_2}\right)^2  |  \frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC} \quad (DE \parallel BC)
When to use: Similar triangles formed by parallel lines or angle-angle congruence.
⚡ Shortcut: Area ratio $49 : 81 \implies$ Side ratio $= 7 : 9$, Perimeter ratio $= 7 : 9$, Altitude ratio $= 7 : 9$.
🚨 Trap: Confusing side ratio with area ratio. Area ratio is ALWAYS the square of the linear scale factor!
📐 Circle Angle Properties & Alternate Segment
\angle BOC = 2\angle BAC  |  \text{Semicircle} = 90^\circ  |  \angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ  |  \angle(\text{tangent, chord}) = \angle(\text{alternate segment})
When to use: Angles subtended by chords, tangents, and inscribed quadrilaterals.
⚡ Shortcut: Angle in semicircle is $90^\circ$. Whenever diameter is given, immediately connect endpoints to circumference to create a right angle!
🚨 Trap: Alternate segment theorem angle is opposite to the chord, NOT the adjacent interior angle.
📐 Interior & Exterior Angle Bisector Theorems
\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}  |  AD^2 = AB \cdot AC - BD \cdot DC  |  \frac{BE}{EC} = \frac{AB}{AC} \text{ (exterior)}
When to use: Triangle with an angle bisector dividing the opposite side or exterior extended line.
⚡ Shortcut: The segments on base divide in the EXACT ratio of the adjacent legs: $BD : DC = c : b$.
🚨 Trap: Swapping $BD/DC$ as $AC/AB$. Segment $BD$ is adjacent to side $AB$, so it corresponds to $AB$ in numerator!
📐 Right-Angled Triangle Metric Relations
BD = \frac{AB \times BC}{AC}  |  BD^2 = AD \times DC  |  AB^2 = AD \times AC  |  m_{\text{hyp}} = \frac{AC}{2} = R
When to use: Altitude dropped from $90^\circ$ vertex onto hypotenuse.
⚡ Shortcut: Median from right angle to hypotenuse equals HALF of hypotenuse ($m = c/2 = R$). If legs are $6, 8 \implies$ median to hypotenuse $= 5$ cm!
🚨 Trap: Assuming altitude equals median. Altitude $\perp$ hypotenuse; median bisects hypotenuse. They coincide only in isosceles right triangles.
📐 Tangential Quadrilateral & Ptolemy's Theorem
\text{Tangential Quad: } AB + CD = BC + AD  |  \text{Ptolemy: } AC \cdot BD = (AB \cdot CD) + (BC \cdot AD)
When to use: Circle inscribed in a quadrilateral (touching 4 sides), or cyclic quadrilateral diagonals.
⚡ Shortcut: Sum of opposite sides is EQUAL: $Top + Bottom = Left + Right$. E.g. $8 + 12 = 9 + x \implies x = 11$ in 2 seconds!
🚨 Trap: Applying $AB + CD = BC + AD$ to cyclic quad instead of tangential quad. Tangential = circle inside; Cyclic = circle outside!

⚡ Speed Shortcuts

⚡ 1. Core Pythagorean Triplets Arsenal
Instant visual recognition: $(3,4,5)$, $(5,12,13)$, $(7,24,25)$, $(8,15,17)$, $(9,40,41)$, $(11,60,61)$, $(12,35,37)$, $(20,21,29)$. Scaled multiples: $(15,20,25)$, $(10,24,26)$, $(16,30,34)$. Solves 80% of altitude/chord questions without square roots!
⚡ 2. Tangential Quadrilateral 3-Second Equation
A circle touches all $4$ sides of $ABCD$: $AB + CD = BC + AD$. E.g., $AB = 8.4, BC = 9.8, CD = 5.6 \implies AD = (8.4 + 5.6) - 9.8 = 14.0 - 9.8 = 4.2$ cm in 3 seconds!
⚡ 3. Incenter Angle Quick Ladder
$\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac{\angle A}{2}$. If $\angle A = 70^\circ \implies 90 + 35 = 125^\circ$. If $\angle BIC = 135^\circ \implies \angle A/2 = 45^\circ \implies \angle A = 90^\circ$ (Right-angled triangle!).
⚡ 4. Right Triangle Altitude to Hypotenuse
In right $\Delta ABC$ ($ngle B = 90^\circ$), altitude $BD = \frac{\text{Base} \times \text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{AB \times BC}{AC}$. If legs are $15$ and $20$, hyp $= 25 \implies BD = \frac{15 \times 20}{25} = 12$ cm in 5 seconds!
⚡ 5. Tangent-Secant Chord Hack
Given $PT = 15$ and $PA = 9$: $PB = \frac{PT^2}{PA} = \frac{225}{9} = 25$. Chord $AB = PB - PA = 25 - 9 = 16$ cm without quadratic equation!
⚡ 6. Common Tangents Comparison (DCT vs TCT)
$DCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2}$, $TCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2}$. Direct tangent is ALWAYS longer than transverse tangent. If circles touch externally ($d = r_1 + r_2$), $TCT = 0$ and $DCT = 2\sqrt{r_1 r_2}$!

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library

🚨 Trap 1: Tangent-Secant $PB$ vs $AB$ Blunder Error-R
$PT^2 = PA \times PB$. Students often write $PT^2 = PA \times AB$. Fatal error! $PB$ is the TOTAL secant distance from $P$ to the far intersection $B$. The chord is $AB = PB - PA$.
🚨 Trap 2: Area Ratio vs Side/Perimeter Ratio Error-C
Area ratio $= (\text{Side ratio})^2$. If area ratio is $64 : 121$, the side and perimeter ratios are $\sqrt{64/121} = 8 : 11$, NOT $64 : 121$! Don't forget the square root.
🚨 Trap 3: Circumcenter vs Incenter Angle Swapping Error-F
Circumcenter angle is $2A$. Incenter angle is $90^\circ + A/2$. For $A = 70^\circ$: $\angle BOC = 140^\circ$, while $\angle BIC = 125^\circ$. Confusing center types leads directly to trap options.
🚨 Trap 4: Direct vs Transverse Tangent Signs Error-K
DCT has $(r_1 - r_2)^2$. TCT has $(r_1 + r_2)^2$. Using $(+)$ in DCT formula produces an artificially small tangent length. Remember: Direct = Difference (-).
🚨 Trap 5: Angle Bisector Segment Inversion Error-S
In $\Delta ABC$ with bisector $AD$, $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}$. Candidates mistakenly flip it as $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AC}{AB}$. $BD$ touches side $AB$, so it aligns with $AB$.
🚨 Trap 6: Tangential Quad vs Cyclic Quad Mix-up Error-T
$AB + CD = BC + AD$ applies ONLY when a circle is INSCRIBED inside the quadrilateral (tangential). It does NOT apply to cyclic quadrilaterals (circle outside).

Level: Basic FoundationIncenter Angle Formula
Q1: In $\Delta ABC$, $I$ is the incenter of the triangle. If $\angle BAC = 80^\circ$, find the measure of $\angle BIC$.
Hint: Incenter angle formula: $\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac{\angle A}{2}$.
Answer: $130^\circ$.
Step-by-step:
1. $\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac{\angle A}{2} = 90^\circ + \frac{80^\circ}{2}$.
2. $\angle BIC = 90^\circ + 40^\circ = 130^\circ$.
Tag if wrong: [F] Used 2A instead of 90 + A/2 | [C] Addition arithmetic error
Level: Foundation-ModerateCircumcenter & Orthocenter
Q2: In $\Delta ABC$, $O$ is the circumcenter and $H$ is the orthocenter. If $\angle A = 55^\circ$, find the measure of $\angle BOC$ and $\angle BHC$.
Hint: Circumcenter $\angle BOC = 2A$. Orthocenter $\angle BHC = 180^\circ - A$.
Answer: $\angle BOC = 110^\circ$; $\angle BHC = 125^\circ$.
Step-by-step:
1. Circumcenter: $\angle BOC = 2\angle A = 2 \times 55^\circ = 110^\circ$.
2. Orthocenter: $\angle BHC = 180^\circ - \angle A = 180^\circ - 55^\circ = 125^\circ$.
Tag if wrong: [K] Swapped circumcenter and orthocenter formulas | [C] Subtraction slip
Level: Moderate SSC CoreTangent-Secant Theorem
Q3: From an external point $P$, a tangent $PT$ and a secant $PAB$ are drawn to a circle. If $PT = 15$ cm and $PA = 9$ cm, find the length of the chord $AB$.
Hint: $PT^2 = PA \times PB$. Find $PB$, then chord $AB = PB - PA$.
Answer: $16$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Tangent-Secant Theorem: $PT^2 = PA \times PB$.
2. $15^2 = 9 \times PB \implies 225 = 9 \times PB \implies PB = \frac{225}{9} = 25$ cm.
3. Length of chord $AB = PB - PA = 25 - 9 = 16$ cm.
Tag if wrong: [R] Reported PB (25 cm) as chord AB | [C] Division error
Level: ModerateSimilar Triangles Area Ratio
Q4: $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$. The area of $\Delta ABC$ is $64\text{ cm}^2$ and the area of $\Delta DEF$ is $121\text{ cm}^2$. If $EF = 15.4$ cm, find the length of corresponding side $BC$.
Hint: $\frac{\text{Area}_1}{\text{Area}_2} = \left(\frac{BC}{EF}\right)^2 \implies \frac{BC}{EF} = \sqrt{\frac{64}{121}} = \frac{8}{11}$.
Answer: $11.2$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Side ratio: $\frac{BC}{EF} = \sqrt{\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ABC)}{\text{Area}(\Delta DEF)}} = \sqrt{\frac{64}{121}} = \frac{8}{11}$.
2. $BC = \frac{8}{11} \times EF = \frac{8}{11} \times 15.4 = 8 \times 1.4 = 11.2$ cm.
Tag if wrong: [C] Forgot to take square root of areas | [C] Decimal multiplication slip
Level: Moderate SSC CoreDirect Common Tangent (DCT)
Q5: Two circles of radii $9$ cm and $4$ cm have their centers $13$ cm apart. Find the length of their Direct Common Tangent (DCT).
Hint: $DCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2} = \sqrt{13^2 - (9 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25}$.
Answer: $12$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Formula: $DCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2}$.
2. $r_1 - r_2 = 9 - 4 = 5$ cm.
3. $DCT = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm.
⚡ Triplet: $(5, 12, 13)$ triplet solves it immediately!
Tag if wrong: [K] Used (r1 + r2) instead of (r1 - r2)
Level: Moderate-HighTransverse Common Tangent (TCT)
Q6: Two circles with radii $7$ cm and $3$ cm have their centers $26$ cm apart. Find the length of their Transverse Common Tangent (TCT).
Hint: $TCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2} = \sqrt{26^2 - (7 + 3)^2} = \sqrt{676 - 100}$.
Answer: $24$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Formula: $TCT = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2}$.
2. $r_1 + r_2 = 7 + 3 = 10$ cm.
3. $TCT = \sqrt{26^2 - 10^2} = \sqrt{676 - 100} = \sqrt{576} = 24$ cm.
⚡ Triplet: $(10, 24, 26) = 2 \times (5, 12, 13)$ triplet!
Tag if wrong: [K] Subtracted radii instead of adding for TCT
Level: ModerateInterior Angle Bisector
Q7: In $\Delta ABC$, $AD$ is the internal bisector of $\angle A$, meeting $BC$ at point $D$. If $AB = 12$ cm, $AC = 15$ cm, and $BC = 18$ cm, find the length of segment $BD$.
Hint: $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{12}{15} = \frac{4}{5}$. Divide $BC = 18$ in $4 : 5$ ratio.
Answer: $8$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Angle Bisector Theorem: $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{12}{15} = \frac{4}{5}$.
2. Total parts $= 4 + 5 = 9$ parts $\implies 9$ parts $= 18$ cm $\implies 1$ part $= 2$ cm.
3. $BD = 4 \times 2 = 8$ cm. (And $DC = 5 \times 2 = 10$ cm).
Tag if wrong: [S] Inverted ratio to 5:4 (getting 10 cm) | [C] Arithmetic error

Hint: For a quadrilateral circumscribing a circle: $AB + CD = BC + AD$.
Answer: $4.2$ cm.
Step-by-step:
1. Theorem: $AB + CD = BC + AD$.
2. $8.4 + 5.6 = 9.8 + AD$.
3. $14.0 = 9.8 + AD \implies AD = 14.0 - 9.8 = 4.2$ cm.

Q9: In a right-angled triangle $ABC$, $\angle B = 90^\circ$, $AB = 15$ cm and $BC = 20$ cm. An altitude $BD$ is drawn from vertex $B$ perpendicular to hypotenuse $AC$. Find the length of $BD$.
Hint: Hypotenuse $AC = \sqrt{15^2 + 20^2} = 25$ cm. $BD = \frac{AB \times BC}{AC}$.
Answer: $12$ cm.

1. Hypotenuse $AC$: By $(15, 20, 25)$ triplet $= 5 \times (3, 4, 5) \implies AC = 25$ cm.
2. Metric Altitude Formula: $BD = \frac{AB \times BC}{AC} = \frac{15 \times 20}{25}$.

Q10: In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, sides $AB$ and $DC$ when extended meet at point $P$, and sides $AD$ and $BC$ when extended meet at point $Q$. If $\angle A = 60^\circ$ and $\angle P = 30^\circ$, find the measure of $\angle Q$.
Hint: In $\Delta ADP$, exterior $\angle C = \angle A + \angle P = 60 + 30 = 90^\circ$. Then in $\Delta ABQ$...
Answer: $30^\circ$.
Step-by-step:
1. In cyclic quad $ABCD$, opposite angles sum to $180^\circ \implies \angle C = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$.
2. Consider $\Delta PBC$: Exterior angle at $D$ is $\angle BCD + \angle P$... In $\Delta ADP$: $\angle ADP = 180 - (60 + 30) = 90^\circ$.
3. Since $\angle ADC + \angle ADP = 180^\circ \implies \angle ADC = 90^\circ$.
4. In cyclic quad, $\angle B = 180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$.
5. In $\Delta ABQ$: $\angle A + \angle B + \angle Q = 180^\circ \implies 60^\circ + 90^\circ + \angle Q = 180^\circ \implies \angle Q = 30^\circ$.
Tag if wrong: [C] Angle sum arithmetic slip | [K] Cyclic quad supplementary property

SSC CGL 2023 Tier-1 (Shift 2) Difficulty: Moderate
Question: In $\Delta ABC$, $D$ and $E$ are points on sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively such that $DE \parallel BC$. If $AD : DB = 3 : 5$ and the area of $\Delta ADE = 45\text{ cm}^2$, find the area of quadrilateral $BDEC$.
✅ Correct Answer: $275\text{ cm}^2$

⚡ 15-Second Area Ratio Trick:
• Side ratio $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{3}{3 + 5} = \frac{3}{8}$.
• Area ratio $\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{Area}(\Delta ABC)} = \left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{64}$.
• Area of quadrilateral $BDEC = 64 - 9 = 55$ units.
• We are given: $9$ units $= 45\text{ cm}^2 \implies 1$ unit $= 5\text{ cm}^2$.
• Area of $BDEC = 55 \times 5 = 275\text{ cm}^2$!

🚨 Examiner Trap: Squaring $5$ directly as $25$ units and calculating $25 \times 5 = 125\text{ cm}^2$. $AB$ is $3 + 5 = 8$, so the full triangle is $8^2 = 64$, not $5^2$!
SSC CGL 2022 Tier-2 (Mains) Difficulty: Moderate
Question: From a point $P$ outside a circle, $PAB$ is a secant and $PT$ is a tangent to the circle. If $PT = 8$ cm and $PA = 6$ cm, find the length of the chord $AB$.
✅ Correct Answer: $4\frac{2}{3}$ cm (or $\frac{14}{3}$ cm $\approx 4.67$ cm)

⚡ Direct Theorem Application:
• $PT^2 = PA \times PB \implies 8^2 = 6 \times PB \implies PB = \frac{64}{6} = \frac{32}{3}$ cm.
• Chord $AB = PB - PA = \frac{32}{3} - 6 = \frac{32 - 18}{3} = \frac{14}{3} = 4\frac{2}{3}$ cm.
SSC CGL 2023 Tier-1 (Shift 1) Difficulty: High Level
Question: Two circles of radii $15$ cm and $12$ cm intersect each other, and the length of their common chord is $18$ cm. What is the distance between their centers?
✅ Correct Answer: $12 + 3\sqrt{7}$ cm

⚡ Pythagorean Decomposition:
• Common chord is bisected perpendicularly by line joining centers: Half-chord $= 18/2 = 9$ cm.
• For Circle 1 ($r_1 = 15$ cm): $d_1 = \sqrt{15^2 - 9^2} = \sqrt{225 - 81} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm.
• For Circle 2 ($r_2 = 12$ cm): $d_2 = \sqrt{12^2 - 9^2} = \sqrt{144 - 81} = \sqrt{63} = 3\sqrt{7}$ cm.
• Distance between centers $= d_1 + d_2 = 12 + 3\sqrt{7}$ cm.
SSC CGL 2021 Tier-2 (Mains) Difficulty: High Level
Question: In a circle with center $O$, $AC$ and $BD$ are two chords that meet at point $E$ when produced outside the circle. If $AB$ is the diameter and $ngle AEB = 36^\circ$, find the measure of $ngle DOC$.
✅ Correct Answer: $108^\circ$

⚡ 10-Second Circle Angle Property:
• Since $AB$ is diameter, angle subtended by $AB$ at center is $180^\circ$.
• Formula for angle formed by secants intersecting outside: $ngle E = \frac{\text{Angle}(AB) - \text{Angle}(CD)}{2}$.
• $36^\circ = \frac{180^\circ - \angle DOC}{2} \implies 72^\circ = 180^\circ - \angle DOC$.
• $\angle DOC = 180^\circ - 72^\circ = 108^\circ$!
SSC CGL 2022 Tier-1 Difficulty: Easy
Question: In $\Delta ABC$, $\angle B = 90^\circ$ and $D$ is the midpoint of hypotenuse $AC$. If $AB = 12$ cm and $BC = 16$ cm, find the length of median $BD$.
✅ Correct Answer: $10$ cm

⚡ 3-Second Median to Hypotenuse Property:
• In right $\Delta ABC$, $AC = \sqrt{12^2 + 16^2} = 20$ cm (via $3:4:5$ triplet scaled by $4$).
• Midpoint of hypotenuse is the circumcenter $O$. Therefore, $BD = AD = DC = \frac{AC}{2}$.
• $BD = \frac{20}{2} = 10$ cm!

1. What are the formulas for angles subtended at Incenter (I), Circumcenter (O), and Orthocenter (H)?
Incenter: angle BIC = 90° + A/2. Circumcenter: angle BOC = 2A. Orthocenter: angle BHC = 180° - A.
2. What is the Tangent-Secant theorem formula for a tangent PT and secant PAB?
PT² = PA * PB (where PB = PA + chord AB, the total distance from P to the far point).
3. What are the formulas for Direct Common Tangent (DCT) and Transverse Common Tangent (TCT)?
DCT = sqrt(d² - (r1 - r2)²). TCT = sqrt(d² - (r1 + r2)²).
4. In a right-angled triangle, what is the length of the median drawn from the 90° vertex to the hypotenuse?
Median to hypotenuse = Hypotenuse / 2 = Circumradius R.
5. If two similar triangles have an area ratio of 49 : 81, what is the ratio of their perimeters and altitudes?
Perimeter and altitude ratios are both sqrt(49/81) = 7 : 9.
6. What relation holds between opposite sides when a circle is inscribed in quadrilateral ABCD?
AB + CD = BC + AD (Sum of opposite sides is equal).
7. What is the Interior Angle Bisector Theorem in ΔABC for bisector AD meeting BC at D?
BD / DC = AB / AC (Segments of base are proportional to adjacent sides).
8. In a right-angled triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c, what is the length of altitude to hypotenuse?
Altitude = (a * b) / c.
