💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Incenter Formula
Q: In ΔABC, internal bisectors of ∠B and ∠C meet at I. If ∠A = 70°, find ∠BIC.
Solution:
∠BIC = 90° + ∠A/2 = 90° + 70°/2 = 90° + 35° = 125°.
Circle Tangent-Secant
Q: From an external point P, a tangent PT of length 12 cm is drawn to a circle. A secant PAB passes through center such that PA = 8 cm. Find chord AB length.
Solution:
Tangent-Secant Theorem: PT² = PA × PB
12² = 8 × PB → 144 = 8 × PB → PB = 18 cm.
Chord AB = PB - PA = 18 - 8 = 10 cm.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: Find the number of diagonals in a regular polygon of 12 sides.
Optimal Solution:
Diagonals = n(n-3)/2 = 12(12-3)/2 = 12 × 9 / 2 = 54.
