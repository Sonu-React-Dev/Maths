📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Basic Average
Average = Total Sum / Number of Elements
💡 Total = Average × Count
Replacement Delta
New Member = Old Member + (Total Count × Change in Avg)
💡 Use - if average decreases
Consecutive Series Average
Average = (First Term + Last Term) / 2
💡 Valid for any Arithmetic Progression (AP)

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & Exam Traps

⚡ Deviation Method

Calculate deviations from an estimated average. New Average = Assumed Base + (Sum of Deviations / Total Count).

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Replacement Model
Q: The average weight of 15 students increases by 1.5 kg when one student weighing 40 kg is replaced by another. Find the new student's weight.
Solution:
New = Old + (N × Δ) = 40 + (15 × 1.5) = 40 + 22.5 = 62.5 kg.
Cricket Average
Q: A batsman has a certain average in 11 innings. In the 12th inning, he scores 90 runs and his average increases by 5 runs. Find his new average.
Solution:
Let old avg = A.
12th score = A + 12 × 5 = 90
A + 60 = 90 → A = 30.
New Average = 30 + 5 = 35 runs.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: The average of 9 consecutive numbers is 20. Find the largest number.
Optimal Solution:
In 9 consecutive numbers, average is the 5th (middle) number.
5th number = 20.
Largest (9th number) = 20 + 4 = 24.
