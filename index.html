⚡ 2. Divisibility Rules (विभाज्यता नियम)

Divisor Condition (Short Rule)
2, 4, 8 Last 1 digit (÷2), Last 2 digits (÷4), Last 3 digits (÷8)
3 & 9 Sum of digits divisible by 3 or 9 respectively
5 & 10 Unit digit 0 or 5 (for 5), Unit digit 0 (for 10)
6 Divisible by both 2 AND 3
11 | (Sum of Odd place digits) − (Sum of Even place digits) | = 0 or 11, 22...
72 Check for 8 (last 3 digits) AND 9 (sum of digits)
88 Check for 8 (last 3 digits) AND 11 (odd-even diff)
SSC PYQ Pattern (Rule of 72):
Agar 9-digit number 83P93678Q 72 se divisible hai, to pehle 8 ka rule lagao: 78Q ÷ 8 ⟹ 784 ÷ 8 divisible hota hai, to Q = 4. Fir 9 ka rule lagakar P nikalo!

📐 3. HCF & LCM Core Concepts

HCF × LCM = Product of Two Numbers (A × B)
(Note: Yeh formula sirf aur sirf 2 numbers ke liye valid hai!)
Fraction ka HCF = HCF of Numerators / LCM of Denominators
Fraction ka LCM = LCM of Numerators / HCF of Denominators
⚡ Ratio Trick for Numbers:
Agar do numbers ka ratio a : b hai aur HCF = H hai:
• Pehla number = H × a
• Doosra number = H × b
• LCM = H × a × b

🔢 4. Number of Factors (गुणनखंडों की संख्या)

Agar kisi number $N$ ka prime factorization hai:
N = a^p × b^q × c^r (jahan a, b, c primes hain):

Total Factors = (p + 1)(q + 1)(r + 1)
Sum of Factors = [(a^(p+1) - 1)/(a - 1)] × [(b^(q+1) - 1)/(b - 1)] ...
Example: N = 72
72 = 8 × 9 = 2³ × 3²
Total Factors = (3 + 1) × (2 + 1) = 4 × 3 = 12.
Number of Odd Factors = (powers of odd primes + 1) = (2 + 1) = 3.
Number of Even Factors = Total - Odd = 12 - 3 = 9.

🔄 5. Unit Digit & Power Cyclicity (इकाई अंक)

Kisi bhi power ka unit digit nikalne ke liye power ko 4 se divide karke remainder dekhte hain:

0, 1, 5, 6
Cycle Length = 1
Hamesha wahi number rehta hai
4, 9
Cycle Length = 2
Odd/Even power rule
2, 3, 7, 8
Cycle Length = 4
Power ÷ 4 Remainder
Rem = 0 ?
Power = 4 maano
Agar poora kat jaye
Example: Find the unit digit of 7^105:
Power = 105. 105 ÷ 4 = Remainder 1.
Isliye Unit digit = 7¹ = 7. ✅

📝 6. SSC CGL Practice PYQs (Collapsible Answers)

Q1. Do sankhyaon ka ratio 3 : 4 hai aur unka HCF = 4 hai. Unka LCM kya hoga? (SSC CGL)
Solution:
Numbers = 3 × 4 = 12, aur 4 × 4 = 16.
LCM = HCF × a × b = 4 × 3 × 4 = 48. ✅
Q2. Agar 7-digit number 5432y47 9 se poori tarah divisible hai, to y ka maan kya hoga?
Solution:
Sum of digits = 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + y + 4 + 7 = 25 + y.
25 ke baad 9 se katne wali sankhya = 27.
25 + y = 27 ⟹ y = 2. ✅
Q3. Sankhya 120 ke kul kitne dhangatmak gunankhand (positive factors) hain?
Solution:
120 ka prime factorization: 120 = 2³ × 3¹ × 5¹.
Total Factors = (3 + 1)(1 + 1)(1 + 1) = 4 × 2 × 2 = 16. ✅
