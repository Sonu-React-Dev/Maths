📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Golden CP/MP Formula
CP / MP = (100 - Discount %) / (100 + Profit %)
💡 Use (100 - Loss %) if loss
Successive Discount
D_net = d₁ + d₂ - (d₁ × d₂) / 100
💡 For 3 discounts, apply iteratively
Dishonest Dealer Profit
Profit % = [(True Value - False Value) / False Value] × 100
💡 Base is always False (Given) Value
Buy X Get Y Free
Discount % = [Y / (X + Y)] × 100
💡 Total items given in denominator

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & Exam Traps

⚡ SP Equality Shortcut

Two items sold at same SP, one at +x% profit and other at -x% loss → Overall loss is always (x/10)² %.

⚡ Dishonest Multiplier Chain

Har fraud ka multiplying factor likho aur multiply karo: SP/CP = (Claimed SP/CP) × (True Weight / False Weight).

🚨 Discount Base Trap

Discount hamesha Marked Price (MP) par calculate hota hai, CP par nahi!

🚨 Dishonest Base Trap

Agar 1 kg ke badle 800g deta hai, profit (200/1000) = 20% NAHI hai; it is (200/800) = 25%!

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Markup-Discount Chain
Q: A shopkeeper marks goods 40% above CP and allows 20% discount. Find his profit %.
Solution:
CP = 100 → MP = 140.
Discount = 20% of 140 = 28.
SP = 140 - 28 = 112.
Profit = 12%.
Or Net = +40 - 20 - (40×20)/100 = 20 - 8 = +12%.
Golden Formula
Q: A trader gives 10% discount on MP and earns 20% profit. If MP is ₹800, find CP.
Solution:
Golden Formula: CP / MP = (100 - D) / (100 + P) = (100 - 10) / (100 + 20) = 90 / 120 = 3/4.
CP = (3/4) × 800 = ₹600.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: A dishonest dealer claims to sell at cost price but uses a weight of 900 gm for 1 kg. Find his profit percentage.
Optimal Solution:
Profit % = [(1000 - 900) / 900] × 100 = (100 / 900) × 100 = 11.11% (11 1/9 %).
