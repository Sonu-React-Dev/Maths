📐 Formula Vault

📐 Multiply by 5
N × 5 = (N ÷ 2) × 10
When to use: Any number — even: 84×5=420; odd: 87×5=435
⚡ Shortcut: Halve the number, append zero
🚨 Trap: Odd numbers: remainder 0.5 × 10 = 5 at end
📐 Multiply by 25
N × 25 = (N ÷ 4) × 100
When to use: N divisible by 4 is fastest
⚡ Shortcut: Divide by 4, add two zeros
🚨 Trap: If N not divisible by 4, decimal appears — handle carefully
📐 Multiply by 125
N × 125 = (N ÷ 8) × 1000
When to use: N divisible by 8
⚡ Shortcut: Divide by 8, add three zeros
🚨 Trap: For 72×125: 72÷8=9 → 9000
📐 Near-100 Base Multiplication
(100−a)(100−b) = [100−a−b] | [a×b]
When to use: Both numbers close to 100
⚡ Shortcut: 96×97: deficit 4,3 → (96−3)|(4×3) = 93|12 = 9312
🚨 Trap: If a×b > 99, carry over to left part
📐 Square of n5 numbers
(A5)² = [A×(A+1)] | 25
When to use: Any number ending in 5
⚡ Shortcut: 65²: 6×7=42 → 4225; 85²: 8×9=72 → 7225
🚨 Trap: Always append 25, not just 5

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library

🚨 BODMAS 'OF' Trap Error-C
20 ÷ 5 of 2 = 20 ÷ (5×2) = 20 ÷ 10 = 2. 'OF' (का) = multiplication, executes BEFORE division!
🚨 Double Negative Sign Trap Error-S
-(-a) = +a. Example: -7 − (−12) = -7 + 12 = +5, NOT −19.
🚨 Mixed Fraction Division Trap Error-C
2½ ÷ 1¼: Convert to improper FIRST (5/2 ÷ 5/4), THEN multiply by reciprocal = 5/2 × 4/5 = 2. Never divide mixed fractions directly.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab

🔐 Integrity Rule: Actual PYQ tabhi label karo jab year/shift verified ho. Otherwise: "PYQ-style" label use karo.
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern
Simplify: 18 ÷ [6 − {4 − (8 − 6 + 3 of 2)}]
3 of 2=6 → 8−6+6=8 → 4−8=−4 → 6−(−4)=10 → 18÷10 = 1.8
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
Find the value of: (0.1)³ + (0.2)³ + (0.3)³ − 3(0.1)(0.2)(0.3) / [(0.1)² + (0.2)² + (0.3)² − (0.1)(0.2) − (0.2)(0.3) − (0.3)(0.1)]
Uses a³+b³+c³−3abc = (a+b+c)[(a²+b²+c²−ab−bc−ca)]. So result = a+b+c = 0.1+0.2+0.3 = 0.6
