📐 Formula Vault

📐 Key 2D Formulas
Rhombus: Area=d₁d₂/2 | Side: 4a²=d₁²+d₂² Equilateral Δ: Area=√3/4 a² | Inrad=a/2√3 | Circumrad=a/√3 Trapezium: Area=½(a+b)h Sector: Area=(θ/360)πr² | Arc=θ/360×2πr
When to use: 2D shape area/perimeter calculation
⚡ Shortcut: Rhombus side from diagonals: half-diagonals form a right triangle with side as hypotenuse.
🚨 Trap: Rhombus perimeter=4a (NOT 2(d₁+d₂))! Area=d₁d₂/2 (NOT d₁+d₂).
📐 Key 3D Formulas
Cylinder: V=πr²h | CSA=2πrh | TSA=2πr(r+h) Cone: V=⅓πr²h | l=√(r²+h²) | CSA=πrl Sphere: V=⁴⁄₃πr³ | SA=4πr² Hemisphere: V=⅔πr³ | TSA=3πr²
When to use: 3D shape volume and surface area problems
⚡ Shortcut: Hemisphere TSA = Curved(2πr²) + Base(πr²) = 3πr². Many students forget the circular base.
🚨 Trap: Hemisphere TSA=3πr² NOT 2πr². Curved surface only = 2πr².
📐 Recasting / Melting Rule
Volume₁ = Volume₂ (material conserved) n × V_small = V_large n = V_large / V_small
When to use: Any melting, recasting, reshaping problem
⚡ Shortcut: Sphere R=6 melted to spheres r=2: n=(6/2)³=27.
🚨 Trap: Volume is conserved ONLY if problem doesn't mention wastage/loss. If 10% loss is mentioned, V₂=0.9V₁.

⚡ Speed Shortcuts

⚡ Sphere-to-Spheres Count Formula
n small spheres from 1 large: n = (R/r)³. Cylinder-to-spheres: n = πR²H/(4/3πr³). Set volumes equal and solve.
⚡ Painting Painted Area Shortcut
Cube painted on all sides, cut into n³ small cubes: 3 faces painted=(n-2)³×8corners. Adjust for 2-face, 1-face, 0-face. Formula: corners=8, edges=12(n-2), faces=6(n-2)², interior=(n-2)³.
⚡ Diagonal Formulas
Square diagonal=a√2. Cuboid space diagonal=√(l²+b²+h²). Cube space diagonal=a√3.

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library

🚨 Hemisphere TSA = 3πr² Trap Error-F
Hemisphere's Total Surface Area = Curved surface (2πr²) + Circular base (πr²) = 3πr². Many students use 2πr² (forgetting the base).
🚨 Cone Slant Height Not Height Error-C
Cone CSA = πrl where l = SLANT height = √(r²+h²). Using h (vertical height) instead of l gives wrong CSA.
🚨 Recasting Wastage Trap Error-R
If problem says 10% material is wasted during recasting: V_products = 0.9 × V_original. Without this adjustment, count of objects will be wrong.

Diagonals of a rhombus are 24 cm and 10 cm. Find its perimeter.
Hint: Half-diagonals form right triangle with side as hypotenuse.
Answer: Side = √(12²+5²) = √(144+25) = √169 = 13 cm. Perimeter=4×13=52 cm.

Level B: Standard
A cylinder of radius 6 cm and height 8 cm is melted to form a cone of radius 6 cm. Find height of cone.
Hint: Same radius: Volume cylinder = πr²×8. Volume cone = (1/3)πr²×h. Set equal.
Answer: V_cylinder = V_cone → πr²h₁ = ⅓πr²h₂ → h₂ = 3h₁ = 24 cm.

Hint: Sector area = (θ/360)×πr². Use θ=120°, r=21.
Answer: A = (120/360)×π×21² = (1/3)×(22/7)×441 = 462 cm².

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab

🔐 Integrity Rule: Actual PYQ tabhi label karo jab year/shift verified ho. Otherwise: "PYQ-style" label use karo.
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-1
A hemispherical bowl of radius 9 cm is to be painted inside. Find the cost at ₹4 per cm².
Curved SA of hemisphere = 2πr² = 2×(22/7)×81 = 509.14 cm². Cost = 509.14×4 ≈ ₹2,036.
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-1
If radius of sphere is doubled, by what % does its volume increase?
V ∝ r³. New V = (2r)³/r³ = 8 times. Increase = (8-1)/1 × 100 = 700%.
