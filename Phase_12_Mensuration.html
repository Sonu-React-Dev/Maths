📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Rhombus Area & Side
Area = (d₁ × d₂) / 2 | 4a² = d₁² + d₂²
💡 Diagonals bisect at 90°
Cylinder CSA & TSA
CSA = 2πrh | TSA = 2πr(r + h) | Volume = πr²h
💡 Base area = πr²
Cone CSA & Volume
Volume = (1/3)πr²h | CSA = πrl (where l = √(r²+h²))
💡 TSA = πr(l + r)
Sphere vs Hemisphere
Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, TSA = 4πr² | Hemisphere: TSA = 3πr²
💡 Hemisphere CSA = 2πr²

⚡ Recasting Count Formula

Number of small spheres formed = Volume of Large Solid / Volume of 1 Small Sphere = (R / r)³.

🚨 Hemisphere Total Surface Area Trap

Hemisphere ka TSA 2πr² NAHI hai; curved surface + circular base = 2πr² + πr² = 3πr²!

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Recasting Formula
Q: A solid metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 2 cm. Find number of small spheres formed.
Solution:
n = (R / r)³ = (6 / 2)³ = 3³ = 27 spheres.
Rhombus Properties
Q: The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Find its perimeter.
Solution:
Side a = √[(d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²] = √[8² + 6²] = √100 = 10 cm.
Perimeter = 4a = 4 × 10 = 40 cm.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: If radius of a cylinder is doubled and height is halved, find the ratio of new volume to original volume.
Optimal Solution:
V₁ = π r² h.
V₂ = π (2r)² (h/2) = π (4r²) (h/2) = 2 π r² h = 2 V₁.
Ratio = 2 : 1.
