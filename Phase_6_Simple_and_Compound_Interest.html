📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Simple Interest
SI = (P × R × T) / 100
💡 Amount = P + SI
2-Year CI - SI Difference
D₂ = P × (R / 100)²
💡 Direct formula for 2 years
3-Year CI - SI Difference
D₃ = P × (R / 100)² × [(300 + R) / 100]
💡 Or D₃ = 3×D₂ + P(R/100)³
CI Equal Installment
P = [x / (1 + R/100)] + [x / (1 + R/100)²]
💡 x = amount of each installment

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & Exam Traps

⚡ CI Ratio Method

If R = 10% = 1/10, Principal : Amount for 2 yrs = 10² : 11² = 100 : 121. CI = 21 units.

⚡ Sum Doubles Shortcut

In SI: If sum becomes n times in t yrs, it becomes m times in t × (m-1)/(n-1) yrs.

🚨 Compounding Period Rate Trap

Half-yearly compounding mein Rate ko aadha (R/2) aur Time ko double (2T) karna kabhi mat bhoolna!

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Difference Formula
Q: The difference between CI and SI on a sum at 10% p.a. for 2 years is ₹125. Find the sum.
Solution:
D₂ = P(R/100)²
125 = P(10/100)² = P(1/100)
P = ₹12,500.
Constant Multiplier
Q: A sum of money amounts to ₹6690 after 3 years and ₹10035 after 6 years on CI. Find the principal.
Solution:
Since time doubles (3 yrs to 6 yrs), ratio remains constant:
A₂ / A₁ = 10035 / 6690 = 3/2.
So A₁ / P = 3/2 → 6690 / P = 3/2 → P = (6690 × 2) / 3 = ₹4,460.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: In how many years will a sum of money double itself at 12.5% per annum simple interest?
Optimal Solution:
SI = P → (P × 12.5 × T) / 100 = P
T = 100 / 12.5 = 8 years.
