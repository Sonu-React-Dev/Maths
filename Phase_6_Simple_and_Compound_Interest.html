📐 Formula Vault

📐 Simple Interest
SI = (P × R × T) / 100 | Amount = P + SI
When to use: Rate is constant, interest simple (not compounded)
⚡ Shortcut: Sum doubles at rate R in T years: T = 100/R years (SI). So at 8%, doubles in 12.5 years.
🚨 Trap: In SI, interest each year is same. In CI, interest grows each year. Don't mix them.
📐 CI − SI Difference (2 Years)
D₂ = P × (R/100)²
When to use: Same P, same R, 2-year period. D₂ = CI₂ − SI₂.
⚡ Shortcut: P=10000, R=10%: D₂ = 10000×(1/10)² = ₹100. SI₂=2000, CI₂=2100.
🚨 Trap: This formula only works for 2 years with SAME P and R starting from year 1.
📐 CI − SI Difference (3 Years)
D₃ = P(R/100)² × (300+R)/100 OR D₃ = D₂(3 + R/100)
When to use: Same P, same R, 3-year period
⚡ Shortcut: P=10000, R=10%: D₂=100, D₃=100×(3+0.1)=310.
🚨 Trap: Don't use 2-year formula for 3 years. D₃ > 3×D₂ always!
📐 Half-Yearly Compounding
A = P(1 + R/200)^(2T) where R is annual rate
When to use: Compounding twice a year
⚡ Shortcut: 10% annual half-yearly for 2 years = (1+5%)⁴ = 1.2155. Effective annual rate ≈ 10.25%.
🚨 Trap: R becomes R/2 (half the annual rate) but T becomes 2T (double the time periods).

⚡ Speed Shortcuts

⚡ Ratio Method for CI
R=10%=1/10. P:A for 1yr=10:11, 2yr=100:121, 3yr=1000:1331. CI = difference. For 2yr: CI=21units per 100 invested.
⚡ Sum Doubles/Triples in SI
Doubles at R% in (100/R) years. Triples in (200/R) years. Becomes n-times in [(n-1)×100/R] years.
⚡ Standard Effective Rates
10% for 2yr: SI=20%, CI=21%. For 3yr: CI=33.1%. 5% for 2yr: SI=10%, CI=10.25%. Memorize these!

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library

🚨 Half-Yearly Rate Confusion Error-C
Half-yearly compounding at 10% per annum means 5% per HALF-year, not 10%. Rate halves, periods double.
🚨 CI−SI Formula Years Mismatch Error-C
D₂ = P(R/100)² is for EXACTLY 2 years only. Don't apply to 3 years — use D₃ formula.
🚨 Installment Amount Confusion Error-C
In installment problems, each installment pays BOTH principal and interest. Don't just sum installments for total principal.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab

🔐 Integrity Rule: Actual PYQ tabhi label karo jab year/shift verified ho. Otherwise: "PYQ-style" label use karo.
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-1
The CI on a sum for 2 years is ₹410 and SI is ₹400. Find the rate of interest.
SI₂=400 → SI per year=200. D₂=CI−SI=10. D₂=P(R/100)²=P×(R/100)². Also P=SI/(R×T/100)=200×100/R=20000/R. So (20000/R)×(R/100)²=10 → 20000×R/10000=10 → 2R=10 → R=5%.
PYQ-style • SSC CGL Tier-2
A sum is invested at 5% CI compounded annually. After 3 years, interest is ₹1576.25. Find the sum.
A=P(1.05)³=P×1.157625. Interest=0.157625P=1576.25 → P=₹10,000.
