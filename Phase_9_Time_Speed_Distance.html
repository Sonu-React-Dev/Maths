📐 Formula Vault

📐 Fundamental Equations & Unit Staircase
\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}  |  \text{km/h} \times \frac{5}{18} = \text{m/s}  |  \text{m/s} \times \frac{18}{5} = \text{km/h}
When to use: Every single TSD question. Units must be uniform (km, hr, km/h OR m, s, m/s).
⚡ Shortcut: Multiples of 18 km/h: $18=5$ m/s, $36=10$ m/s, $54=15$ m/s, $72=20$ m/s, $90=25$ m/s, $108=30$ m/s. Instant conversions!
🚨 Trap: Multiplying minutes directly with km/h without converting minutes to hours ($\div 60$).
📐 Average Speed (Harmonic Mean & Weighted)
\text{Equal Distances: } V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 S_1 S_2}{S_1 + S_2}  |  \text{General: } V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\sum D_i}{\sum \frac{D_i}{S_i}}
When to use: Round trips, or journeys split into equal/unequal segments at varying speeds.
⚡ Shortcut: 3 equal distances: $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{3 S_1 S_2 S_3}{S_1 S_2 + S_2 S_3 + S_3 S_1}$. Or assume distance as LCM of speeds!
🚨 Trap: Using arithmetic average $\frac{S_1 + S_2}{2}$. Average speed for equal distance is strictly the Harmonic Mean!
📐 Early & Late Speed Difference Theorem
\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \times S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \Delta t  \text{ (hours)}
When to use: Person travels at speed $S_1$ and arrives late/early, then at $S_2$ and arrives early/late.
⚡ Shortcut: If both conditions are same (late-late or early-early): $\Delta t = |t_1 - t_2|$. If opposite (late-early): $\Delta t = t_1 + t_2$.
🚨 Trap: Forgetting to divide time difference $\Delta t$ in minutes by 60 to convert to hours!
📐 Relative Speed & Train Crossing Mechanics
S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 \pm S_2  |  \text{Distance} = L_1 + L_2  |  T_{\text{cross}} = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{S_{\text{rel}}}
When to use: Trains crossing poles, platforms, pedestrians, or each other.
⚡ Shortcut: Distance covered when two trains cross is ALWAYS $L_1 + L_2$ regardless of whether they move in opposite or same direction!
🚨 Trap: Subtracting lengths ($L_1 - L_2$) when moving in the same direction. Distance is always the SUM of lengths!
📐 Boats & Streams Equations
D = u + v  |  U = u - v  |  u = \frac{D + U}{2}  |  v = \frac{D - U}{2}  |  \frac{u}{v} = \frac{T_u + T_d}{T_u - T_d}
When to use: Boats, swimmers, airplanes with headwind/tailwind. $u = $ still water speed, $v = $ stream speed.
⚡ Shortcut: If upstream takes $3\times$ longer than downstream for same distance ($T_u/T_d = 3/1$): $\frac{u}{v} = \frac{3+1}{3-1} = 2:1$ in 3 seconds!
🚨 Trap: $D$ must ALWAYS be strictly greater than $U$. If $D \le U$, the boat cannot move upstream ($u > v$ always).
📐 After-Meeting Speed Theorem
\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}}  |  t_{\text{meet}} = \sqrt{T_1 \times T_2}
When to use: Two objects start simultaneously from A and B toward each other, cross each other at M, and take $T_1$ and $T_2$ hours to complete the remaining journey.
⚡ Shortcut: Notice the inversion: $\frac{S_1}{S_2}$ corresponds to $\sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}}$. If $T_1 = 4$ and $T_2 = 9$, $\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{9/4} = 3/2$!
🚨 Trap: Forgetting to take the square root or swapping $T_1$ and $T_2$ in the fraction.
📐 Circular Track Races & Headstart
T_{\text{first meet}} = \frac{L}{S_{\text{rel}}}  |  T_{\text{meet at start}} = \text{LCM}\left(\frac{L}{S_1}, \frac{L}{S_2}\right)
When to use: Circular running tracks of circumference $L$, and linear races where A beats B by $x$ meters or $t$ seconds.
⚡ Shortcut: In linear race: If A beats B by $x$ meters or $t$ seconds, Speed of B $= \frac{x}{t}$ directly!
🚨 Trap: Confusing "meeting anywhere on the track" with "meeting at the original starting point".

⚡ Speed Shortcuts

⚡ 1. Unit Staircase Multiples of 18
Direct memory recall: $18 \text{ km/h} = 5$ m/s, $36 = 10$, $54 = 15$, $72 = 20$, $90 = 25$, $108 = 30$ m/s. Avoid manual multiplication by $5/18$ during exams to save 15 seconds per question!
⚡ 2. 5-Second Early & Late Distance
$\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \times S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \frac{\Delta t}{60}$. If walks at $4$ km/h reaches $10$ min late, at $5$ km/h reaches $5$ min early $\implies \Delta t = 15$ min $= 1/4$ h. Distance $= \frac{4 \times 5}{1} \times \frac{1}{4} = 5$ km flat!
⚡ 3. Stoppage Time per Hour Shortcut
$\text{Stoppage Time/hr} = \frac{\text{Speed without stop} - \text{Speed with stop}}{\text{Speed without stop}} \times 60 \text{ min}$. E.g., $54$ km/h and $45$ km/h $\implies \frac{9}{54} \times 60 = 10$ minutes/hour!
⚡ 4. Train Platform vs Pole Difference
Train crosses a pole in $12$s and a $300$m platform in $27$s. The extra $27 - 12 = 15$s covers ONLY the platform! Speed $= 300 / 15 = 20$ m/s ($72$ km/h). Train length $= 20 \times 12 = 240$m in 8 seconds!
⚡ 5. After-Meeting Instant Ratio
When two trains cross and take $T_1$ and $T_2$ hours to complete their journeys: $\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}}$. If $T_1 = 4$ and $T_2 = 9$, then $S_1 : S_2 = 3 : 2$. If $S_1 = 60 \implies S_2 = 40$ km/h!
⚡ 6. Police-Thief Catching Distance
Initial gap $= d$. Thief speed $= v_1$, Cop speed $= v_2$. Time to catch $= \frac{d}{v_2 - v_1}$. Total distance run by policeman $= v_2 \times \frac{d}{v_2 - v_1} = d \times \frac{v_2}{v_2 - v_1}$. Instant 1-step multiplier!

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library

🚨 Trap 1: Arithmetic Mean Average Speed Error-C
A car travels at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. Candidate writes $(60+40)/2 = 50$ km/h. Wrong! Average speed for equal distance is harmonic mean: $\frac{2 \times 60 \times 40}{100} = 48$ km/h.
🚨 Trap 2: Early/Late Sign Inversion Error-R
"10 min late" and "4 min late": Both are late, so difference is $10 - 4 = 6$ min. Adding $10 + 4 = 14$ min ruins the entire distance calculation. Add ONLY when opposite (early + late).
🚨 Trap 3: Subtracting Train Lengths in Same Direction Error-F
In relative motion, speeds subtract in the same direction, but train lengths ALWAYS ADD ($L_1 + L_2$). Never subtract train lengths! Both bodies must clear each other completely.
🚨 Trap 4: Circular Track Meeting Distinction Error-K
"Time to meet for the first time anywhere" is $\frac{L}{|S_1 - S_2|}$. "Time to meet at the STARTING point" is $LCM(L/S_1, L/S_2)$. They are two completely different questions.
🚨 Trap 5: Minutes to Hours Unit Neglect Error-T
When speeds are in km/h and time differences are in minutes, multiplying speed by minutes directly produces an answer 60 times too large! Always convert minutes to hours: $\text{mins} / 60$.
🚨 Trap 6: Swapping Upstream and Downstream Speeds Error-S
Downstream is WITH the current ($u + v$). Upstream is AGAINST the current ($u - v$). $D$ is always greater than $U$. Inverting them makes stream speed negative.

💡 Guided Practice Questions (One at a Time)

Question 1 of 10
Level: Basic FoundationUnit Conversion & Pole Crossing
Q1: A $180$-meter long train crosses a telegraph pole in $9$ seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
Hint: Speed in m/s $= 180 / 9 = 20$ m/s. Convert to km/h by multiplying by $\frac{18}{5}$.
Answer: $72$ km/h.
Step-by-step:
1. Speed in m/s $= \frac{\text{Length of train}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{180}{9} = 20$ m/s.
2. Speed in km/h $= 20 \times \frac{18}{5} = 4 \times 18 = 72$ km/h.
⚡ Direct: From 18-table, $20$ m/s $= 4 \times 5$ m/s $= 4 \times 18 = 72$ km/h immediately.
Tag if wrong: [C] Multiplied by 5/18 instead of 18/5 | [F] Unit formula inverted
Level: FoundationAverage Speed (Equal Distances)
Q2: A motorist travels from town A to town B at a speed of $40$ km/h and returns from B to A along the same route at $60$ km/h. What is his average speed for the entire round trip?
Hint: Since distances are equal, $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 \times 40 \times 60}{40 + 60}$. Do NOT take $(40+60)/2$!
Answer: $48$ km/h.
Step-by-step:
1. Harmonic mean formula: $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 S_1 S_2}{S_1 + S_2} = \frac{2 \times 40 \times 60}{40 + 60}$.
2. $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{4800}{100} = 48$ km/h.
Tag if wrong: [C] Answered 50 km/h (arithmetic mean trap) | [C] Calculation slip
Level: Moderate SSC CoreEarly and Late Theorem
Q3: Walking at $5$ km/h, a student reaches his school $15$ minutes late. If he increases his speed to $6$ km/h, he is still $5$ minutes late. Find the distance from his house to the school.
Hint: Both cases are LATE, so $\Delta t = 15 - 5 = 10$ minutes $= 10/60 = 1/6$ hour. Use $\frac{S_1 S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \Delta t$.
Answer: $5$ km.
Step-by-step:
1. Time difference: $\Delta t = 15 - 5 = 10$ minutes $= \frac{10}{60} = \frac{1}{6}$ hour.
2. Distance formula: $D = \frac{S_1 \times S_2}{S_2 - S_1} \times \Delta t = \frac{5 \times 6}{6 - 5} \times \frac{1}{6}$.
3. $D = 30 \times \frac{1}{6} = 5$ km.
Tag if wrong: [R] Added times (15+5=20) instead of subtracting | [T] Forgot to divide by 60
Level: ModerateStoppage Time per Hour
Q4: Excluding stoppages, the speed of an express bus is $64$ km/h, and including stoppages, its speed drops to $48$ km/h. For how many minutes does the bus stop per hour on average?
Hint: Formula: $\frac{\text{Without stop} - \text{With stop}}{\text{Without stop}} \times 60 = \frac{64 - 48}{64} \times 60$.
Answer: $15$ minutes per hour.
Step-by-step:
1. Stoppage time per hour $= \frac{S_{\text{fast}} - S_{\text{slow}}}{S_{\text{fast}}} \times 60$ minutes.
2. $= \frac{64 - 48}{64} \times 60 = \frac{16}{64} \times 60 = \frac{1}{4} \times 60 = 15$ minutes.
Tag if wrong: [C] Divided by 48 instead of 64 | [F] Inverted stoppage ratio
Level: Moderate-HighTrain Crossing Platform & Man
Q5: A train crosses a platform $200$ m long in $20$ seconds, and passes a man standing on the platform in $12$ seconds. Find the length of the train and its speed in km/h.
Hint: The extra $20 - 12 = 8$ seconds is spent covering ONLY the $200$m platform! Speed $= 200/8 = 25$ m/s.
Answer: Length $= 300$ m; Speed $= 90$ km/h.
Step-by-step:
1. Time to cover $200$ m platform alone $= 20 - 12 = 8$ seconds.
2. Speed of train $= \frac{200}{8} = 25$ m/s.
3. Speed in km/h $= 25 \times \frac{18}{5} = 90$ km/h.
4. Length of train $= \text{Speed} \times \text{Time to cross man} = 25 \times 12 = 300$ m.
Tag if wrong: [C] Arithmetic error in 200/8 | [S] Calculated length using 20s
Level: Moderate-HighTwo Trains in Opposite Direction
Q6: Two trains of lengths $150$ m and $170$ m are running in opposite directions on parallel tracks at speeds of $40$ km/h and $32$ km/h respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
Hint: Distance $= 150 + 170 = 320$ m. Relative speed $= 40 + 32 = 72$ km/h $= 20$ m/s.
Answer: $16$ seconds.
Step-by-step:
1. Total Distance $= L_1 + L_2 = 150 + 170 = 320$ m.
2. Relative Speed (Opposite) $= 40 + 32 = 72$ km/h.
3. Convert to m/s: $72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 20$ m/s.
4. Time taken $= \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Relative Speed}} = \frac{320}{20} = 16$ seconds.
Tag if wrong: [C] Subtracted speeds instead of adding | [C] Addition error in lengths
Level: ModerateBoats & Streams Core
Q7: A motorboat travels $36$ km downstream in $2$ hours and covers the same distance upstream in $3$ hours. Find the speed of the motorboat in still water and the speed of the river current.
Hint: Downstream $D = 36/2 = 18$ km/h. Upstream $U = 36/3 = 12$ km/h. $u = (D+U)/2, v = (D-U)/2$.
Answer: Boat speed $= 15$ km/h; Stream speed $= 3$ km/h.
Step-by-step:
1. Downstream Speed $D = \frac{36}{2} = 18$ km/h.
2. Upstream Speed $U = \frac{36}{3} = 12$ km/h.
3. Boat speed in still water $u = \frac{D + U}{2} = \frac{18 + 12}{2} = 15$ km/h.
4. Stream speed $v = \frac{D - U}{2} = \frac{18 - 12}{2} = 3$ km/h.
Tag if wrong: [F] Subtracted for boat speed | [C] Arithmetic division error
Level: High Level (Mains Pattern)After-Meeting Speed Theorem
Q8: Two trains P and Q start at the same time from Delhi and Patna towards each other. After meeting, they take $4$ hours $48$ minutes and $3$ hours $20$ minutes respectively to reach Patna and Delhi. If train P is moving at $45$ km/h, find the speed of train Q.
Hint: $T_1 = 4\frac{48}{60} = \frac{24}{5}$ hrs. $T_2 = 3\frac{20}{60} = \frac{10}{3}$ hrs. Apply $\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}}$.
Answer: $54$ km/h.
Step-by-step:
1. Convert times to fractions: $T_1 = 4\frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{5}$ h; $T_2 = 3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}$ h.
2. Theorem: $\frac{S_P}{S_Q} = \sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}} = \sqrt{\frac{10/3}{24/5}} = \sqrt{\frac{10 \times 5}{3 \times 24}} = \sqrt{\frac{50}{72}} = \sqrt{\frac{25}{36}} = \frac{5}{6}$.
3. Since $S_P = 45$ km/h $\implies \frac{45}{S_Q} = \frac{5}{6} \implies S_Q = \frac{45 \times 6}{5} = 54$ km/h.
Tag if wrong: [F] Used T1/T2 inside root without inverting | [C] Fraction simplification error
Level: High LevelLinear Race Compounding
Q9: In a $1000$-meter race, A beats B by $100$ meters. In a $400$-meter race, B beats C by $40$ meters. By how many meters will A beat C in a $500$-meter race?
Hint: Ratio $A : B = 1000 : 900 = 10 : 9$. Ratio $B : C = 400 : 360 = 10 : 9$. Combine to find $A : C$.
Answer: $95$ meters.
Step-by-step:
1. When A runs $1000$ m, B runs $900$ m $\implies \frac{A}{B} = \frac{10}{9}$.
2. When B runs $400$ m, C runs $360$ m $\implies \frac{B}{C} = \frac{10}{9}$.
3. Combine: $\frac{A}{C} = \frac{A}{B} \times \frac{B}{C} = \frac{10}{9} \times \frac{10}{9} = \frac{100}{81}$.
4. In a $500$ m race: When A runs $500$ m ($100 \times 5$), C runs $81 \times 5 = 405$ m.
5. Winning margin $= 500 - 405 = 95$ meters.
Tag if wrong: [C] Multiplied by wrong factor | [R] Added deficits (10% + 10% = 20%)
Level: Advanced Tier-2Circular Track Double Query
Q10: Two runners A and B run on a circular track of circumference $600$ m in the same direction. The speed of A is $10$ m/s and the speed of B is $6$ m/s. After how many seconds will they meet for the first time anywhere on the track, and after how many seconds will they meet for the first time at the starting point?
Hint: First meet $= \frac{L}{S_A - S_B} = \frac{600}{10 - 6}$. Meet at start $= LCM(L/S_A, L/S_B) = LCM(60, 100)$.
Answer: First meet anywhere $= 150$ s; At start point $= 300$ s.
Step-by-step:
1. First meeting anywhere: $T_{\text{meet}} = \frac{L}{S_A - S_B} = \frac{600}{10 - 6} = \frac{600}{4} = 150$ seconds.
2. Meeting at the starting point:
    Lap time for A $= \frac{600}{10} = 60$ seconds.
    Lap time for B $= \frac{600}{6} = 100$ seconds.
    Meet at start $= LCM(60, 100) = 300$ seconds.
Tag if wrong: [K] Confused first meet with meet at start | [C] LCM calculation slip

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab — Real Exam Questions with Examiner Traps

SSC CGL 2023 Tier-1 (Shift 3) Difficulty: Moderate
Question: A person travels a total distance of $240$ km in $6$ hours, partly by bus at $30$ km/h and partly by train at $50$ km/h. What is the distance traveled by train?
✅ Correct Answer: $150$ km

⚡ 12-Second Alligation on Speed:
• Overall average speed $= \frac{240}{6} = 40$ km/h.
• Bus speed $= 30$, Train speed $= 50$, Mean speed $= 40$.
• Ratio of Time taken: $(50 - 40) : (40 - 30) = 10 : 10 = 1 : 1$.
• Total time $= 6$ hours $\implies$ Time by train $= 3$ hours.
• Distance by train $= 50 \text{ km/h} \times 3 \text{ h} = 150$ km!

🚨 Examiner Trap: Assuming $1:1$ is the distance ratio! Alligation on speed gives TIME ratio ($T_{\text{bus}} : T_{\text{train}}$). Distance must be calculated using $S \times T$.
SSC CGL 2022 Tier-2 (Mains) Difficulty: Moderate
Question: A train takes $18$ seconds to pass completely through a station platform $162$ m long and $15$ seconds to pass through another platform $120$ m long. Find the length of the train.
✅ Correct Answer: $90$ m

⚡ 10-Second Delta Method:
• Difference in distance $= 162 - 120 = 42$ m.
• Difference in time $= 18 - 15 = 3$ seconds.
• Speed of train $= \frac{42}{3} = 14$ m/s.
• Total distance in $15$ s $= 14 \times 15 = 210$ m.
• Length of train $= 210 - 120 = 90$ m!
SSC CGL 2023 Tier-1 (Shift 1) Difficulty: Advanced
Question: A boat goes $30$ km upstream and $44$ km downstream in $10$ hours. In $13$ hours, it can go $40$ km upstream and $55$ km downstream. Determine the speed of the boat in still water.
✅ Correct Answer: $8$ km/h

⚡ HCF Factor Inspection Trick:
• Look at downstream distances: $44$ and $55$. Their common factor is $11$. Try $D = 11$ km/h.
• If $D = 11$: Downstream time in Case 1 is $44/11 = 4$ hours.
• Upstream time in Case 1 $= 10 - 4 = 6$ hours $\implies U = \frac{30}{6} = 5$ km/h.
• Verify Case 2: $\frac{40}{5} + \frac{55}{11} = 8 + 5 = 13$ hours. Perfectly satisfied!
• Speed of boat in still water $u = \frac{D + U}{2} = \frac{11 + 5}{2} = 8$ km/h.
• Solved in 15 seconds without setting up simultaneous equations!
SSC CGL 2021 Tier-2 (Mains) Difficulty: Moderate-High
Question: Two persons A and B start moving from two points X and Y towards each other simultaneously. After meeting, they take $3$ hours $12$ minutes and $1$ hour $48$ minutes respectively to reach Y and X. If the speed of A is $9$ km/h, then the speed of B is:
✅ Correct Answer: $12$ km/h

⚡ Direct Theorem Application:
• $T_A = 3\text{h } 12\text{m} = 3\frac{1}{5} = \frac{16}{5}$ hours.
• $T_B = 1\text{h } 48\text{m} = 1\frac{4}{5} = \frac{9}{5}$ hours.
• $\frac{S_A}{S_B} = \sqrt{\frac{T_B}{T_A}} = \sqrt{\frac{9/5}{16/5}} = \sqrt{\frac{9}{16}} = \frac{3}{4}$.
• $\frac{9}{S_B} = \frac{3}{4} \implies S_B = \frac{9 \times 4}{3} = 12$ km/h.
SSC CGL 2022 Tier-1 Difficulty: Moderate
Question: A thief is spotted by a policeman from a distance of $200$ m. When the policeman starts the chase, the thief also starts running. If the speed of the thief is $10$ km/h and that of the policeman is $11$ km/h, at what distance from the start of the chase will the policeman catch the thief?
✅ Correct Answer: $2.2$ km (or $2,200$ m)

⚡ 8-Second Proportionality Shortcut:
• Speed ratio Policeman : Thief $= 11 : 10$.
• Since time of chase is same, Distance ratio $= 11 : 10$.
• Difference between them is $11 - 10 = 1$ unit.
• This $1$ unit represents the initial gap of $200$ m.
• Distance run by Policeman $= 11 \times 200 = 2,200$ m $= 2.2$ km!
• (Distance run by Thief $= 10 \times 200 = 2,000$ m).

1. What is the formula for average speed over two equal distances at speeds S1 and S2?
V_avg = (2 * S1 * S2) / (S1 + S2) (Harmonic Mean).
2. What is the After-Meeting speed theorem connecting speeds and remaining travel times?
S1 / S2 = sqrt(T2 / T1). Meeting time t_meet = sqrt(T1 * T2).
3. When two trains move in the SAME direction on parallel tracks, what is the distance covered to cross each other?
ALWAYS the SUM of lengths (L1 + L2). Distance never subtracts!
4. In Boats and Streams, how do you find boat speed (u) and stream speed (v) from Downstream (D) and Upstream (U)?
Boat speed u = (D + U) / 2. Stream speed v = (D - U) / 2.
5. What is the shortcut formula for bus stoppage time in minutes per hour?
Stoppage time/hr = [(Speed without stops - Speed with stops) / Speed without stops] * 60 minutes.
6. On a circular track of circumference L, what is the time to meet for the first time anywhere vs at the starting point (same direction)?
Anywhere: L / (S1 - S2). At start point: LCM(L/S1, L/S2).
7. What are the m/s values for 36, 54, 72, and 90 km/h?
36 km/h = 10 m/s; 54 km/h = 15 m/s; 72 km/h = 20 m/s; 90 km/h = 25 m/s.
8. How do you find the distance when someone travels at S1 arriving late by t1, and at S2 arriving early by t2?
Distance = [ (S1 * S2) / |S1 - S2| ] * [ (t1 + t2) / 60 ].
