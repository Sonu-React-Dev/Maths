Day 60 Speed-Distance-Time Basics & Conversions

1 km/h = 5/18 m/s, 1 m/s = 18/5 km/h. Distance proportionality.

Day 61 Average Speed

Equal distance: 2xy/(x+y). General: Total Distance / Total Time.

Day 62 Relative Speed Principles

Same direction: |u - v|. Opposite direction: (u + v). Meeting time = Distance / Relative Speed.

Day 63 Trains Fundamentals

Crossing a pole/man: Distance = Train Length. Crossing platform: Train + Platform Length.

Day 64 Two Trains Crossing & Overtaking

Distance = L₁ + L₂. Relative speed depending on direction.

Day 65 Boats & Streams

Downstream D = u + v, Upstream U = u - v. Boat speed u = (D+U)/2, Stream v = (D-U)/2.

Day 66 Races & Circular Tracks

📐 Formula Vault & Core Logic

Unit Conversion
km/h × (5/18) = m/s | m/s × (18/5) = km/h
💡 Remember: smaller unit (m/s) takes smaller fraction (5/18)
Equal Distance Average Speed
Average Speed = (2 × x × y) / (x + y)
💡 Harmonic mean of speeds
Boats & Streams
Boat Speed u = (D + U) / 2 | Stream Speed v = (D - U) / 2
💡 D = Downstream, U = Upstream
After Meeting Speed Ratio
S₁ / S₂ = √(T₂ / T₁)
💡 T₁, T₂ are times taken after crossing to reach destination

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & Exam Traps

⚡ Early & Late Shortcut

Distance = [ (S₁ × S₂) / |S₁ - S₂| ] × Total Time Difference (in hrs).

⚡ Train Platform Crossing

If train crosses two platforms of length L1, L2 in t1, t2: Speed = (L1 - L2) / (t1 - t2).

🚨 Average Speed Arithmetic Trap

Ek aadmi 60 km/h se gaya aur 40 km/h se lauta. Avg speed (60+40)/2 = 50 NAHI hai; it is 2(60)(40)/(60+40) = 48 km/h!

💡 Solved Foundation Examples

Train Crossing Platform
Q: A train 180 m long running at 72 km/h crosses a platform in 20 seconds. Find platform length.
Solution:
Speed = 72 × (5/18) = 20 m/s.
Total Distance = Speed × Time = 20 × 20 = 400 m.
Platform Length = 400 - 180 = 220 m.
After-Meeting Formula
Q: Two trains start at same time from A and B towards each other. After meeting, they reach destinations in 4 hrs and 9 hrs respectively. If first train speed is 60 km/h, find second train speed.
Solution:
S₁ / S₂ = √(T₂ / T₁) = √(9 / 4) = 3 / 2.
60 / S₂ = 3 / 2 → S₂ = (60 × 2) / 3 = 40 km/h.

🏆 SSC PYQ Lab (Tier 1 & 2)

SSC CGL Tier 1
Q: A man can row 9 km/h in still water. It takes him twice as long to row upstream as downstream. Find stream speed.
Optimal Solution:
u = 9.
T_up / T_down = 2/1 → (u + v) / (u - v) = 2/1.
By componendo-dividendo: u / v = (2+1)/(2-1) = 3/1.
9 / v = 3 → v = 3 km/h.
