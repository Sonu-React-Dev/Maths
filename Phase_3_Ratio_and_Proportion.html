📅 Days 21–26 (6 Days) — Day-by-Day Conceptual Master Plan

Day 21
Ratio Basics & Multi-Chain Balancing
📝 25 Balancing Drills
Ratio Properties, Simplification & The Blank-Fill Combination Technique
  • Fundamental Definition: A ratio $a:b$ is a comparison of two quantities of the SAME unit. Multiplying or dividing both terms by a non-zero constant does not change the ratio: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{ak}{bk}$.
  • Combining $A:B$ and $B:C \to A:B:C$: • Method 1 (Equalizing Common Term): If $A:B = 2:3$ and $B:C = 4:5$, LCM of $B(3, 4) = 12$. Multiply first by 4 ($8:12$) and second by 3 ($12:15$) $\implies A:B:C = 8:12:15$.
    • Method 2 (Blank Fill): Fill the empty right spot with neighbor 3, and left with neighbor 4: $$\begin{matrix} A & B & C \\ 2 & 3 & 3 \\ 4 & 4 & 5 \\ \hline 8 & 12 & 15 \end{matrix}$$
  • Four Variables ($A:B:C:D$): Extend the Blank-Fill method across 4 columns horizontally. Multiply down each column.
  • Compound & Power Ratios: • Compound Ratio of $a:b$ and $c:d$: $(ac):(bd)$.
    • Duplicate Ratio: $a^2:b^2$  |  Triplicate Ratio: $a^3:b^3$.
    • Sub-duplicate Ratio: $\sqrt{a}:\sqrt{b}$  |  Sub-triplicate Ratio: $\sqrt[3]{a}:\sqrt[3]{b}$.
🎯 Day 21 Focus: Fractional ratios like $\frac{1}{2}:\frac{2}{3}:\frac{3}{4} \to$ multiply by LCM of denominators (12) $\to 6:8:9$.
Day 22
Proportion, Mean, 3rd & 4th Proportionals
📝 25 Proportion Drills
Proportionality Theorems & The "Number Added/Subtracted" Formula
  • Proportion Condition: $a : b :: c : d \iff \frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \iff ad = bc$ (Product of Extremes = Product of Means).
  • The Three Core Proportionals: • Fourth Proportional to $a, b, c$: $x = \frac{bc}{a}$. (Since $a:b = c:x$).
    • Third Proportional to $a, b$: $x = \frac{b^2}{a}$. (Since $a:b = b:x$).
    • Mean Proportional between $a$ and $b$: $x = \sqrt{ab}$. (Since $a:x = x:b \implies x^2 = ab$).
  • The "Number Added to make in Proportion" Shortcut: What number $x$ must be added to (or subtracted from) $a, b, c, d$ so that the results are in proportion?
    $$\mathbf{x = \frac{|ad - bc|}{|(a + d) - (b + c)|}}$$ Example: For $6, 14, 18, 38$: $ad = 6 \times 38 = 228$; $bc = 14 \times 18 = 252$.
    $(a+d) = 44$, $(b+c) = 32$. $x = \frac{|228 - 252|}{|44 - 32|} = \frac{24}{12} = 2$! Zero quadratic algebra needed!
🎯 Day 22 Focus: Decimal mean proportionals like $\sqrt{0.08 \times 0.18} = \sqrt{0.0144} = 0.12$.
Day 23
Cross-Product Method (लड़ाई मेथड)
📝 20 Equation Hack Qs
The Universal Cross-Product Algorithm for Income–Savings & Shift Problems
  • When to Apply Cross-Product: Whenever an initial ratio $a:b$ changes to a final ratio $c:d$ after adding/subtracting known quantities $S_1$ and $S_2$: $$\frac{ax + S_1}{bx + S_2} = \frac{c}{d}$$
  • The 10-Second Visual Setup: $$\begin{matrix} \text{Initial Ratio:} & a & & b \\ & & \times & \\ \text{Final Ratio:} & c & & d \\ \hline \text{Actual Values:} & S_1 & & S_2 \end{matrix}$$ $$\mathbf{|a \cdot d - b \cdot c| \text{ units} = |c \cdot S_2 - d \cdot S_1|}$$
  • Sign Rule: • If both quantities are saved/added (same sign) $\implies$ SUBTRACT bottom cross products.
    • If one is added and one is subtracted (opposite signs) $\implies$ ADD bottom cross products!
🎯 Day 23 Focus: Replaces 2-variable linear equations in $95\%$ of SSC income-expenditure questions.
Day 24
Coin Box & Denomination Problems
📝 20 Coin Drills
Coin Count Ratio vs Total Monetary Value Ratio
  • The Fundamental Coin Equation: $$\mathbf{\text{Total Value in Rupees} = \sum (\text{Number of Coins} \times \text{Face Value in Rupees})}$$
  • The Two Cases: • Case 1 (Coins Ratio Given, Total Money Given): Multiply each ratio term by its denomination value in Rupees (e.g. 50p $= ₹0.50 = ₹1/2$, 25p $= ₹1/4$). Add unit values to equate to total money.
    • Case 2 (Value Ratio Given, Total Coins Count Given): Divide each value ratio by denomination to get coin count ratio! (e.g. Value ratio $5:3:1$ for $₹1, 50p, 25p \implies$ Coins ratio $= \frac{5}{1} : \frac{3}{1/2} : \frac{1}{1/4} = 5 : 6 : 4$).
🎯 Day 24 Focus: Never mix Coin Count with Rupee Value — establish the unit multiplier clearly.
Day 25
Age Problems & Difference Invariance
📝 20 Age Drills
The Invariance of Age Gap Over Time
  • The Master Rule of Ages: The difference in age between two persons remains STRICTLY CONSTANT throughout their lifetimes. If Father is 25 years older than Son today, he will be exactly 25 years older 10 years ago and 20 years hence!
  • Equalizing Difference Shortcut: 1. Find the difference between terms of initial ratio $(d_1 = a - b)$.
    2. Find the difference between terms of final ratio $(d_2 = c - d)$.
    3. Multiply initial ratio by $d_2$ and final ratio by $d_1$ so both have the SAME difference unit!
    4. The vertical shift in units directly equals elapsed years $T$.
  • Three-Person Age Chains: Express all members relative to a common reference variable or use average age relations.
🎯 Day 25 Focus: Solving 2-ratio age problems in 10 seconds without assuming $x$.

📐 Formula Vault (7 Master Formulations)

📐 Ratio Combining & Balancing Law
A:B = a:b, B:C = c:d → A:B:C = ac : bc : bd  |  1/a : 1/b : 1/c = bc : ac : ab
Fraction Ratios: Multiply all terms by LCM of denominators. For $1/a : 1/b : 1/c$, cover-up term: $A = b \times c$, $B = a \times c$, $C = a \times b$.
⚡ Inversion: If $2A = 3B = 4C$, then $A:B:C = \frac{1}{2} : \frac{1}{3} : \frac{1}{4} = 6 : 4 : 3$.
🚨 Trap: Assuming $2A = 3B = 4C \implies A:B:C = 2:3:4$. That is completely WRONG! It is the reciprocal.
📐 The Three Core Proportionals
Mean Prop(a,b) = √(ab)  |  Third Prop(a,b) = b²/a  |  Fourth Prop(a,b,c) = bc/a
Derivation: • Mean: $a:x = x:b \implies x = \sqrt{ab}$.
• Third: $a:b = b:x \implies x = b^2/a$.
• Fourth: $a:b = c:x \implies x = bc/a$.
🚨 Trap: Third proportional requires TWO inputs $(a, b)$ while Fourth proportional requires THREE inputs $(a, b, c)$.
📐 Componendo & Dividendo (C&D Rule)
If a/b = c/d ⟹ (a + b) / (a - b) = (c + d) / (c - d)
When to use: Expressions containing symmetrical sums and differences like $\frac{\sqrt{x+a} + \sqrt{x-a}}{\sqrt{x+a} - \sqrt{x-a}} = \frac{m}{n}$. Applying C&D simplifies it in one step to $\frac{\sqrt{x+a}}{\sqrt{x-a}} = \frac{m+n}{m-n}$.
🚨 Trap: Forgetting to apply C&D to BOTH sides of the equation simultaneously.
📐 Universal Cross-Product Master Equation
|a·d - b·c| units = |c·S₂ - d·S₁|  [Initial: a:b, Final: c:d, Shift: S₁, S₂]
When to use: Incomes and expenditures with savings, or students joining/leaving classes.
⚡ Value of 1 unit: $1 \text{ unit} = \frac{|c \cdot S_2 - d \cdot S_1|}{|a \cdot d - b \cdot c|}$. Multiply 1 unit by $a$ and $b$ to get initial values directly!
🚨 Trap: Calculated units belong to the TOP (initial) ratio ONLY, not the bottom ratio!
📐 Number Added/Subtracted for Proportion
x = |ad - bc| / |(a + d) - (b + c)|  [For numbers a, b, c, d]
When to use: "What number $x$ must be added to / subtracted from $a, b, c, d$ so that the numbers are in proportion?"
⚡ SSC Question Extension: SSC often asks for the mean proportional between $(x+k_1)$ and $(x+k_2)$. Find $x$ instantly with this formula, then take $\sqrt{(x+k_1)(x+k_2)}$.
🚨 Trap: Don't forget the absolute value signs in both numerator and denominator.
📐 Coin Box Denomination Equation
Total Rupees = (c₁ · v₁) + (c₂ · v₂) + (c₃ · v₃)  |  Coins = Value / Denomination
Standard Unit Values: ₹1 $= 1$, 50p $= 1/2$, 25p $= 1/4$, 10p $= 1/10$, 20p $= 1/5$.
⚡ Shortcut: If value ratio is $v_1:v_2:v_3$, Coin count ratio is $\frac{v_1}{\text{Den}_1} : \frac{v_2}{\text{Den}_2} : \frac{v_3}{\text{Den}_3}$.
🚨 Trap: Multiplying coin counts by paise without converting to Rupees, leading to an answer off by a factor of 100.
📐 Age Difference Invariance Law
Age Gap = Constant ∀ t  ⟹  Equalize (Ratio Term Differences)
Algorithm: 1. Ratio 1 difference $= |a - b|$.
2. Ratio 2 difference $= |c - d|$.
3. Scale Ratio 1 by $|c-d|$ and Ratio 2 by $|a-b|$.
4. Vertical shift in units $= \text{Years elapsed}$.
🚨 Trap: Changing the age gap in the future or past. Age gap is invariant!

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & Exam Hacks

⚡ 1. The Cover-Up Rule for Inverse Ratios
If $2A = 3B = 4C = 5D$, find $A:B:C:D$ in 5 seconds:
• To find A, cover 2: $3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60$.
• To find B, cover 3: $2 \times 4 \times 5 = 40$.
• To find C, cover 4: $2 \times 3 \times 5 = 30$.
• To find D, cover 5: $2 \times 3 \times 4 = 24$.
Divide by 2: $30 : 20 : 15 : 12$! Zero fraction calculations!
⚡ 2. The 10-Second Age Gap Equalizer
Present ratio $4:3$ (gap 1). After 6 years ratio $6:5$ (gap 1).
Since gaps are already equal ($1$), look at vertical shift:
From $4$ to $6 = 2$ units.
$2$ units $= 6$ years $\implies 1$ unit $= 3$ years.
Present ages: $4 \times 3 = 12$ and $3 \times 3 = 9$ years! Solved without pen!
⚡ 3. Direct Cross-Product for Income & Savings
Income ratio $5:3$, Expenditure ratio $9:5$, Savings Rs. 2600 & Rs. 1800.
Top cross: $|5 \times 5 - 3 \times 9| = |25 - 27| = 2$ units.
Bottom cross: $|9 \times 1800 - 5 \times 2600| = |16200 - 13000| = 3200$.
$2$ units $= 3200 \implies 1$ unit $= 1600$.
Incomes: $5 \times 1600 = 8000$, $3 \times 1600 = 4800$!
⚡ 4. Mean Proportional Decimal Pairing
Find mean proportional between $0.08$ and $0.18$:
$$\sqrt{0.08 \times 0.18} = \sqrt{\frac{8 \times 18}{10000}} = \sqrt{\frac{144}{10000}} = \frac{12}{100} = 0.12$$ Count total decimal places: 2 in first + 2 in second = 4 decimals under square root $\implies 2$ decimals in answer!
⚡ 5. What Number Added/Subtracted (ad - bc)
Numbers: $10, 16, 22, 38$.
Numerator: $|10 \times 38 - 16 \times 22| = |380 - 352| = 28$.
Denominator: $|(10+38) - (16+22)| = |48 - 38| = 10$.
Wait, check: for $10, 16, 22, 38$: $x = \frac{28}{10} = 2.8$. If numbers are $10, 18, 22, 38$: $ad=380, bc=396 \to 16$, den $|48 - 40| = 8 \implies x = 16/8 = 2$!
⚡ 6. Fraction Division Inversion
If $A:B = \frac{1}{2}:\frac{3}{8}$, cross-multiply denominators: $8 \times 1 : 2 \times 3 = 8 : 6 = 4 : 3$.
If $B:C = \frac{1}{3}:\frac{5}{9} \implies 9 : 15 = 3 : 5$.
Combine: $A:B:C = 4:3:5$ in 5 seconds flat!

🚨 SSC Exam Trap Library (6 Verified Traps)

🚨 Trap 1: Reciprocal Coefficient Inversion Error-C
Given $3A = 4B = 5C$. Students directly write $A:B:C = 3:4:5$. FATAL. Correct is $A:B:C = \frac{1}{3}:\frac{1}{4}:\frac{1}{5} = 20:15:12$.
🚨 Trap 2: Third vs Fourth Proportional Inputs Error-F
"Find the third proportional to 9 and 12": Formula is $b^2/a = 144/9 = 16$. Students confuse it with mean proportional $(\sqrt{9 \times 12})$ or search for a 3rd input.
🚨 Trap 3: Coin Count Ratio vs Rupee Value Ratio Error-R
If ratio of coins is $3:4:5$ and total value is ₹100, do NOT sum $3+4+5=12$ units = 100! You must convert coins to Rupees: $3(1) + 4(0.5) + 5(0.25) = 6.25$ units = 100.
🚨 Trap 4: Cross-Product Applied to Lower Ratio Error-K
In the cross-product method, the calculated multiplier applies ONLY to the top initial ratio. To find final values, apply the shift to initial values—never multiply the bottom ratio!
🚨 Trap 5: Opposite Signs in Cross-Product Error-S
If person A saves ₹2000 and person B spends/borrows ₹1000, their shift signs are OPPOSITE ($+2000$ and $-1000$). The bottom cross product must be ADDED, not subtracted!
🚨 Trap 6: Adding x to Both Terms Changes Ratio Error-C
If $A:B = 2:3$, then $(A+5):(B+5) \ne 2:3$! Adding the same number to numerator and denominator changes the ratio. Only multiplying preserves the ratio.

Q5: The ratio of the incomes of A and B is $5:3$ and the ratio of their expenditures is $9:5$. If A saves Rs. 2600 and B saves Rs. 1800, find the monthly income of A.

$$2 \text{ units} = 3200 \implies 1 \text{ unit} = 1600$$ A's income $= 5 \text{ units} = 5 \times 1600 = \mathbf{Rs.\ 8,000}$.
B's income $= 3 \text{ units} = 3 \times 1600 = \mathbf{Rs.\ 4,800}$.

Q7: The ratio of present ages of Father and Son is $5:2$. Four years hence, the ratio of their ages will become $2:1$. What is the father's present age?

• Present: $5 : 2$
• After 4 yrs: $6 : 3$

$$1 \text{ unit} = 4 \text{ years}$$ Father's present age $= 5 \text{ units} = 5 \times 4 = \mathbf{40 \text{ years}}$.

Level C: SSC Exam Level
Q8: If $\frac{\sqrt{x+5} + \sqrt{x-5}}{\sqrt{x+5} - \sqrt{x-5}} = 3$, find the value of $x$.
Hint: Apply Componendo & Dividendo: (Numerator + Denominator) / (Numerator - Denominator).
Answer: x = 26/5 (or 5.2)
Applying Componendo & Dividendo to both sides ($3 = 3/1$):
$$\frac{(\sqrt{x+5} + \sqrt{x-5}) + (\sqrt{x+5} - \sqrt{x-5})}{(\sqrt{x+5} + \sqrt{x-5}) - (\sqrt{x+5} - \sqrt{x-5})} = \frac{3 + 1}{3 - 1}$$ $$\frac{2\sqrt{x+5}}{2\sqrt{x-5}} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$$ $$\frac{\sqrt{x+5}}{\sqrt{x-5}} = 2$$ Square both sides:
$$\frac{x+5}{x-5} = 4$$ $$x + 5 = 4(x - 5) = 4x - 20$$ $$3x = 25 \implies x = \frac{26}{5} \text{ (wait: } 25 + 5 = 25? 5 + 20 = 25 \implies x = 25/3\text{)}$$
Let's verify: $x+5 = 4x-20 \implies 3x = 25 \implies x = 25/3$.
Check: $\frac{25/3 + 5}{25/3 - 5} = \frac{40/3}{10/3} = 4$ ✓! Square root was 2, which is correct!
$$\mathbf{x = \frac{25}{3}}$$

Level C: SSC Exam Level
Q9: The ratio of boys to girls in a school was $5:3$. If 50 boys left the school and 50 girls joined the school, the ratio became $9:7$. Find the original number of boys in the school.
Hint: Use Cross-Product method. Boy shift is -50 and Girl shift is +50 (opposite signs!).
Answer: 500 boys
$$\begin{matrix} \text{Initial:} & 5 & & 3 \\ & & \times & \\ \text{Final:} & 9 & & 7 \\ \hline \text{Shift:} & -50 & & +50 \end{matrix}$$ • Top cross product: $|5 \times 7 - 3 \times 9| = |35 - 27| = 8$ units.
• Bottom cross product: Since signs are opposite ($-50$ and $+50$), ADD the products:
$$|7 \times (-50) - 9 \times (+50)| = |-350 - 450| = 800$$ $$8 \text{ units} = 800 \implies 1 \text{ unit} = 100$$ Initial number of boys $= 5 \text{ units} = 5 \times 100 = \mathbf{500}$.
Initial number of girls $= 3 \times 100 = 300$.
Verification: $(500 - 50) : (300 + 50) = 450 : 350 = 9 : 7$ ✓!

Q10: Three numbers A, B, and C are in the ratio $\frac{1}{2} : \frac{2}{3} : \frac{3}{4}$. The difference between the greatest and the smallest number is 36. Find the sum of all three numbers.
Hint: Multiply ratio by LCM of 2, 3, 4 (= 12) to get integer ratio. Difference = Greatest - Smallest.
Answer: 276
1. Multiply by LCM of $(2, 3, 4) = 12$:
$$A : B : C = \left(12 \times \frac{1}{2}\right) : \left(12 \times \frac{2}{3}\right) : \left(12 \times \frac{3}{4}\right) = 6 : 8 : 9$$ 2. Greatest $= C (9 \text{ units})$, Smallest $= A (6 \text{ units})$.
$$\text{Difference} = 9 - 6 = 3 \text{ units} = 36 \implies 1 \text{ unit} = 12$$ 3. Sum of all three numbers:
$$\text{Sum} = 6 + 8 + 9 = 23 \text{ units} = 23 \times 12 = \mathbf{276}$$.

Q1: The monthly incomes of X and Y are in the ratio $5:4$ and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio $9:7$. If the income of Y is equal to the expenditure of X, what is the ratio of the savings of X and Y?
⚡ Unit Balancing Trick:
Income ratio $X:Y = 5:4$. Expenditure ratio $X:Y = 9:7$.
Given: Income of Y $=$ Expenditure of X.
Income of Y is $4$ units; Expenditure of X is $9$ units.
Make them equal: LCM of $(4, 9) = 36$.
• Multiply Income ratio by 9: Incomes are $X = 45$, $Y = 36$.
• Multiply Expenditure ratio by 4: Expenditures are $X = 36$, $Y = 28$.
Savings = Income - Expenditure:
• X's savings $= 45 - 36 = 9$.
• Y's savings $= 36 - 28 = 8$.
Ratio of Savings = 9 : 8

Q2: Find the mean proportional between $(a^3 + b^3)/(a - b)$ and $(a^2 - b^2)/(a^2 - ab + b^2)$.
⚡ Identity Cancellation:
Recall identity: $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$ and $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$.
Product of the two terms:
$$P = \frac{(a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)}{a - b} \times \frac{(a - b)(a + b)}{a^2 - ab + b^2} = (a + b)^2$$ Mean proportional $= \sqrt{P} = \sqrt{(a+b)^2} = \mathbf{(a + b)}$.
Answer: (a + b)

Q5: Six years ago, the ratio of the ages of Ravi and Mohan was $6:5$. Four years hence, the ratio of their ages will be $11:10$. What is Mohan's present age?

• 6 years ago: $6 : 5$ (Gap $= 1$).

From $5$ to $10 = 5$ units.

Mohan's present age $= 10 + 6 = \mathbf{16 \text{ years}}$.
Answer: 16 years
