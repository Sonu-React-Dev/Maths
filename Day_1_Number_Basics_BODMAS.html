CHAPTER 1

🔢 The Number Family & Basic Classification (संख्याओं का वर्गीकरण)

Maths ki shuruat number systems se hoti hai. Kisi bhi calculation ya algebraic equation ko samajhne se pehle humein numbers ki basic hierarchy pata honi chahiye:

  • 1. Natural Numbers (प्राकृत संख्याएँ — $\mathbb{N}$): Counting numbers jo 1 se shuru hote hain: $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, \dots\}$. Zero is NOT a natural number.
  • 2. Whole Numbers (पूर्ण संख्याएँ — $\mathbb{W}$): Jab Natural numbers ke parivar mein $0$ jud jata hai: $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, \dots\}$. Sabse chhota whole number $0$ hai.
  • 3. Integers (पूर्णांक — $\mathbb{Z}$): Sabhi positive, negative whole numbers: $\{\dots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots\}$.
    • Positive Integers: $\{1, 2, 3, \dots\}$
    • Negative Integers: $\{-1, -2, -3, \dots\}$
    • Zero ($0$): Zero is an integer, but it is neither positive nor negative.
  • 4. Even & Odd Numbers (सम एवं विषम संख्याएँ):
    • Even (सम): Jo $2$ se poori tarah vibhajit ho jaaye ($2k$ form): $\{\dots, -4, -2, 0, 2, 4, 6, \dots\}$. Exam Fact: $0$ is an EVEN number because $0 \div 2 = 0$ with remainder $0$!
    • Odd (विषम): Jo $2$ se vibhajit na ho ($2k \pm 1$ form): $\{\dots, -3, -1, 1, 3, 5, \dots\}$.
  • 5. Rational vs Irrational Numbers (परिमेय बनाम अपरिमेय संख्याएँ):
    • Rational ($p/q$ form): Jahan $p, q$ integers hain aur $q \ne 0$. Inka decimal expansion ya to terminating hota hai (jaise $1/4 = 0.25$) ya repeating recurring hota hai (jaise $1/3 = 0.333\dots$).
    • Irrational: Jinhe $p/q$ mein nahi likha ja sakta (Non-terminating, Non-repeating). Examples: $\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \pi$. (Note: $\frac{22}{7}$ rational hai, lekin actual $\pi$ irrational hai!).
💡 Exam Memory Anchor: Zero se jude 3 golden rules:
1) $0 \div \text{Any non-zero number} = 0$. (Example: $0 \div 7 = 0$).
2) $\text{Any number} \div 0 = \text{Undefined (अपरिभाषित / }\infty)$.
3) $\text{Any non-zero number}^0 = 1$. (Example: $5^0 = 1, (-18)^0 = 1$).
CHAPTER 2

📍 Place Value vs Face Value (स्थानीय मान बनाम जातीय मान)

Yeh topic SSC CGL, CHSL, aur MTS ke Tier-1 ka ek fixed mark-fetcher hai. Dono mein antar samajhna bohot aasan hai:

Face Value (जातीय मान / अंकित मान)

Kisi bhi ank (digit) ka face value wahi sankhya khud hoti hai, chahe wo kisi bhi sthan par ho.
Example: In number $85,429$:
• Face value of $5 = 5$
• Face value of $8 = 8$
• Face value of $4 = 4$

Place Value (स्थानीय मान)

Kisi ank ka sthaniya maan uske position (ikayi, dahai, saikda, hazar) par nirbhar karta hai:
$\mathbf{\text{Place Value} = \text{Digit} \times \text{Position Multiplier}}$
• Place value of $5$ in $85,429 = 5 \times 1000 = 5,000$.
• Place value of $8$ in $85,429 = 8 \times 10000 = 80,000$.

Decimal Numbers mein Place Value (दशमलव संख्याओं का स्थानीय मान):

Decimal point ke daayi (right) taraf place values $\frac{1}{10}$ (tenths), $\frac{1}{100}$ (hundredths), $\frac{1}{1000}$ (thousandths) hoti hain:

Number Component Thousands ($10^3$) Hundreds ($10^2$) Tens ($10^1$) Units ($10^0$) . (Decimal) Tenths ($10^{-1}$) Hundredths ($10^{-2}$) Thousandths ($10^{-3}$)
Digits in 458.729 — 4 (400) 5 (50) 8 (8) . 7 ($0.7$) 2 ($0.02$) 9 ($0.009$)
🚨 SSC Exam Trap Question: "Find the difference between the place value and face value of 7 in 48.729."
Aspirant mistake: Thinking place value is 70 or 7.
Correct Method: Face value $= 7$. Place value $= \frac{7}{10} = 0.7$.
Difference $= 7 - 0.7 = 6.3$.
CHAPTER 3

⚡ Signed Numbers & Operation Rules (चिह्नों के नियम)

Algebra aur Arithmetic dono mein $60\%$ silly errors sirf plus $(+)$ aur minus $(-)$ ke confusion se hote hain. In 4 niyam ko hamesha dhyan me rakhein:

1. Addition & Subtraction Law

  • Same Signs: Numbers ko jodo aur wahi sign lagao.
    $(+7) + (+5) = +12$  |  $(-7) + (-5) = -12$.
  • Opposite Signs: Bade number mein se chhota ghatado aur bade number ka sign lagao.
    $(-9) + (+4) = -5$  |  $(+14) + (-6) = +8$.
  • Double Negative Law: Minus of minus equals plus!
    $15 - (-8) = 15 + 8 = 23$.
    $-10 - (-4) = -10 + 4 = -6$.

2. Multiplication & Division Law

  • $(+) \times (+) = (+)$  •  $6 \times 4 = +24$
  • $(-) \times (-) = (+)$  •  $(-6) \times (-4) = +24$
  • $(+) \times (-) = (-)$  •  $6 \times (-4) = -24$
  • $(-) \times (+) = (-)$  •  $(-6) \times 4 = -24$
  • Division: Same as multiplication:
    $\frac{-36}{-4} = +9$  |  $\frac{-36}{4} = -9$.
📌 Power of Negative Number:
• $(-1)^{\text{EVEN power}} = +1$. Example: $(-1)^{100} = +1$.
• $(-1)^{\text{ODD power}} = -1$. Example: $(-1)^{99} = -1$.
• Careful: $-3^2 = -(3^2) = -9$, but $(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = +9$!
CHAPTER 4

📦 Brackets & Vinculum Hierarchy (कोष्ठक एवं रेखा कोष्ठक)

Brackets ka maksad hota hai operations ko group karna. Jab ek question me multiple brackets ho, to andar se bahar (inside-out) ki taraf solve kiya jata hai:

Priority Order Bracket Name Symbol Rule of Operation
1st Priority (Highest) Vinculum / Bar (रेखा कोष्ठक) $\overline{a - b}$ Sabse pehle bar ke niche ka operation solve hoga! Bar acts as a bracket.
2nd Priority Round Brackets / Parentheses (छोटा कोष्ठक) $( \ )$ Vinculum ke baad round bracket ke andar ki terms simplify karein.
3rd Priority Curly Brackets / Braces (मँझला कोष्ठक) $\{ \ \}$ Round brackets solve hone ke baad curly bracket solve karein.
4th Priority Square Brackets / Box (बड़ा कोष्ठक) $[ \ ]$ Sabse bahar ka bracket jo end me khulta hai.
🚨 The Vinculum Trap (Bar Bracket):
Evaluate: $12 - \overline{7 - 3}$.
Wrong Way: Distributing minus blindly: $12 - 7 - 3 = 2$. ❌
Right Way: Bar ke niche $(7 - 3) = 4$ hai. So $12 - 4 = 8$! ✅
CHAPTER 5

⚙️ Strict VBODMAS Precedence Engine & Deep Examples

Jab ek calculation mein brackets, 'of', divide, multiply, plus aur minus sab ek sath aa jayein, to VBODMAS niyam se hi solve karna compulsory hai:

VBODMAS Hierarchy:
V → Vinculum (Bar: $\overline{x-y}$)
B → Brackets (Inner to outer: $( \ ) \to \{ \ \} \to [ \ ]$)
O → 'Of' (का: Evaluate BEFORE division!)
D → Division ($\div$: Left to right)
M → Multiplication ($\times$: Left to right)
A → Addition ($+$: Left to right)
S → Subtraction ($-$: Left to right)

The Mega Trap: 'Of' (का) vs Multiplication ($\times$):

'Of' ka matlab bhi guna ($\times$) hota hai, lekin 'Of' Division se PEHLE hota hai, jabki aam Multiplication ($\times$) Division ke BAAD hota hai!
• $24 \div 4 \text{ of } 2 = 24 \div (4 \times 2) = 24 \div 8 = \mathbf{3}$.
• $24 \div 4 \times 2 = (24 \div 4) \times 2 = 6 \times 2 = \mathbf{12}$.

Division Chaining (Left-to-Right Rule):

Agar lagatar kai division signs hon, to sabhi subsequent divisors denominator mein multiply ho jate hain: $$\mathbf{a \div b \div c \div d = \frac{a}{b \times c \times d}}$$ Example: $72 \div 6 \div 3 \div 2 = \frac{72}{6 \times 3 \times 2} = \frac{72}{36} = \mathbf{2}$.

📖 Worked Textbook Master Example:

Problem: Evaluate $50 - [20 + \{15 \times 2 - (18 \div 3 \text{ of } 2 + \overline{5 - 2})\}]$.
Step 1 (Vinculum): $\overline{5 - 2} = 3$.
Step 2 ('Of' inside round bracket): $3 \text{ of } 2 = 6 \implies 18 \div 6 = 3$.
Step 3 (Round Bracket): $(3 + 3) = 6$.
Step 4 (Curly Bracket): $\{15 \times 2 - 6\} = \{30 - 6\} = 24$.
Step 5 (Square Bracket): $[20 + 24] = 44$.
Step 6 (Final Subtraction): $50 - 44 = \mathbf{6}$. (Pure, clean, zero error!).

📐 Formula Vault — Completed Cheat Sheet for Day 1

📐 Place Value Decomposition
N = 1000a + 100b + 10c + d
Decimal: $0.abc = \frac{a}{10} + \frac{b}{100} + \frac{c}{1000}$
Exam Use: Diff between place & face value of $d = \text{Place Value} - d$.
📐 'Of' Precedence Law
a ÷ b of c = a ÷ (b × c)
Rule: 'Of' ALWAYS calculates before division!
Contrast: $a \div b \times c = (a/b) \times c$.
📐 Division Chaining Rule
a ÷ b ÷ c ÷ d = a / (b × c × d)
Rule: Collapse all consecutive divisors into the denominator product.
Example: $1000 \div 10 \div 5 \div 2 = 1000 / 100 = 10$.
📐 Negative Power Parity
(-1)^even = +1 | (-1)^odd = -1
Trap: $-a^2 = -(a^2)$ vs $(-a)^2 = +a^2$.
Exam Use: Simplifying alternating sum series.
📐 Vinculum Evaluation Rule
x - [a - b] = x - a + b
Rule: Bar bracket acts as an encapsulated parenthesis.
Example: $10 - \overline{4 - 2} = 10 - 2 = 8$.
📐 Difference of Squares Shortcut
a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b)
Speed Trick: $(8.5^2 - 1.5^2) / (8.5 - 1.5) = 8.5 + 1.5 = 10$.
Avoid: Never calculate square decimals manually!

⚡ Speed Shortcuts & SSC Traps Learned

⚡ 3 Lightning Speed Shortcuts

⚡ 1. Single Denominator Division Collapsing
Jab bhi continuous division mile ($a \div b \div c \div d$), rough sheet par bar-bar division mat karo. Direct $a$ ko numerator me likho aur baaki sabhi terms ko denominator me multiply kardo: $\frac{a}{b \cdot c \cdot d}$. Saves 15 seconds.
⚡ 2. Inside-Out Bracket Scanning
Poori equation dubara copy karne ki jagah, sirf sabse andar ke bracket $(\dots)$ ka value nikal kar uske upar pencil/mental note likho. Ek line me reduction hoti hai!
⚡ 3. Algebraic Identity Decimal Filter
Agar decimal arithmetic jaise $\frac{6.4 \times 6.4 - 3.6 \times 3.6}{6.4 - 3.6}$ dikhe, to multiply mat karo! Yeh hamesha $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$ hota hai, jiska answer direct $6.4 + 3.6 = 10$ hota hai.

🚨 3 Classic SSC Exam Traps

🚨 The "Of vs Divide" BaitError-F
$36 \div 6 \text{ of } 2$: Candidates jaldi me $36 \div 6 = 6$ karke $6 \times 2 = 12$ mark karte hain. Option A hamesha 12 hota hai! Sahi answer $36 \div 12 = 3$ hai.
🚨 The Bar Negative Drop TrapError-S
$15 - \overline{8 - 3}$: Aspirants minus ko dono terms me distribute karke $15 - 8 - 3 = 4$ likh dete hain. Sahi: bar ke andar $8 - 3 = 5$, to $15 - 5 = 10$.
🚨 Decimal Place Value InversionError-R
Number $45.678$ me 7 ka place value $70$ nahi, balki $\frac{7}{100} = 0.07$ hai. Decimal point ke baad tenths, hundredths, thousandths hote hain!

Q1 Place Value & Face Value
Find the difference between the place value and the face value of the digit $7$ in the number $87,543$.
Face value is the digit itself ($7$). Place value depends on its position: $7 \times 1000 = 7000$.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) Face value of digit 7 $= 7$.
2) Position of 7 is at thousands place $\implies \text{Place Value} = 7 \times 1000 = 7000$.
3) Difference $= 7000 - 7 = \mathbf{6,993}$.
Self-rate:
Q2 Sum of Place Values
Find the sum of the place value of $5$ in the number $45,892$ and the place value of $3$ in the number $79,321$.
Identify the position of 5 in the first number, and 3 in the second number. Add the two place values.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) In $45,892$, 5 is at thousands place $\implies \text{Place Value} = 5,000$.
2) In $79,321$, 3 is at hundreds place $\implies \text{Place Value} = 300$.
3) $\text{Sum} = 5000 + 300 = \mathbf{5,300}$.
Self-rate:
Q3 Decimal Place Value
In the decimal number $548.729$, find the place value of the digit $2$.
The first digit after decimal is tenths ($1/10$), the second is hundredths ($1/100$).
Step-by-step Solution:
1) The digit 2 is the second digit to the right of the decimal point.
2) $\text{Place Value} = 2 \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{2}{100} = \mathbf{0.02}$ (two-hundredths).

Q4 Signed Operations
Evaluate the arithmetic expression: $(-18) + (-27) - (-15) + (+10)$.
Remember: minus of minus is plus: $- (-15) = +15$. Group positive and negative terms.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) Simplify double signs: $-18 - 27 + 15 + 10$.
2) Combine negative terms: $-18 - 27 = -45$.
3) Combine positive terms: $+15 + 10 = +25$.
4) Total $= -45 + 25 = \mathbf{-20}$.

Q21 Vinculum + Nested Brackets

Solve bar $\overline{9 - 3} = 6$ first, so $(6 - 6) = 0$.

6) Final: $45 - 18 = \mathbf{27}$.

Q22 Fractional BODMAS
Evaluate: $\frac{3}{4} \text{ of } \frac{8}{9} \div \frac{2}{3} + \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{2}$.
Do 'of' first: $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{2}{3}$. Then divide by $\frac{2}{3}$.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) 'Of' operation: $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{2}{3}$.
2) Division: $\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{2}{3} = 1$.
3) Addition/subtraction: $1 + \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{2} = 1 + \left(\frac{5 - 3}{6}\right) = 1 + \frac{2}{6} = 1 + \frac{1}{3} = \mathbf{\frac{4}{3}}$ (or $1\frac{1}{3}$).

Step-by-step Solution:
1) 'Of': $4 \text{ of } 2 = 8$.
2) Round bracket: $(8 - 6) = 2$.
3) Inside curly brackets: $12 \div 3 \times 2 - 2 = 4 \times 2 - 2 = 8 - 2 = 6$.
4) Square bracket: $[8 - 6] = 2$.
5) Division: $16 \div 2 = \mathbf{8}$.

Q24 Mixed Fraction & 'Of'
Evaluate: $108 \div 36 \text{ of } \frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{5} \times 3\frac{1}{4}$.
Calculate $36 \text{ of } \frac{1}{4} = 9$ first. $3\frac{1}{4} = \frac{13}{4}$.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) 'Of': $36 \text{ of } \frac{1}{4} = 36 \times \frac{1}{4} = 9$.
2) Division: $108 \div 9 = 12$.
3) Multiplication: $\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{13}{4} = \frac{13}{10} = 1.3$.
4) Total $= 12 + 1.3 = \mathbf{13.3}$ (or $\frac{133}{10}$).

Q25 Fraction Vinculum Nested Ladder
Evaluate: $7\frac{1}{2} - [2\frac{1}{4} \div \{1\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2}(1\frac{1}{2} - \overline{\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{6}})\}]$.
Start at the vinculum: $\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{6}$. Work upwards through the brackets.
Step-by-step Solution:
1) Vinculum: $\overline{\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{6}} = \frac{1}{6}$.
2) Inside round bracket: $1\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}$.
3) Multiply by $\frac{1}{2}$: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$.
4) Inside curly bracket: $1\frac{1}{4} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{5}{4} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{15 - 8}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$.
5) Square bracket division: $2\frac{1}{4} \div \frac{7}{12} = \frac{9}{4} \times \frac{12}{7} = \frac{27}{7}$.
6) Final: $\frac{15}{2} - \frac{27}{7} = \frac{105 - 54}{14} = \mathbf{\frac{51}{14}}$ (or $3\frac{9}{14}$).

Q26 Brackets & Multipliers
Evaluate: $25 - 4 \times [18 \div \{12 - (5 - 2)\}] + 7$.
